# Support Hours

> **Category**: company  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

VoltNest support operates on a fixed weekly schedule. This page lists 
standard hours, channel availability, emergency coverage, and holiday 
closures. All times are **US Eastern Time (EST/EDT)**.

## Standard Support Hours

| Day | Hours (EST/EDT) |
|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Tuesday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Wednesday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Thursday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Friday | 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Saturday | 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM |
| Sunday | Closed |

Total weekly coverage: **61 hours**.

## Coverage by Channel

Not every channel is available every hour we're open. Here's the exact 
breakdown:

| Channel | Availability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Live Chat | During standard hours | Fastest response — instant |
| Phone | During standard hours | Instant — for urgent issues |
| Email | 24/7 submission | Replies sent during standard hours |
| Twitter/X | 24/7 submission | Replies sent during standard hours |
| AI Assistant | 24/7 | Available on voltnest.com/chat |

**Important**: Emails and tweets submitted outside standard hours are 
queued and answered in the order received, starting at 9:00 AM EST the 
next business day.

## After-Hours Emergency Support

For **urgent order issues**, we maintain extended monitoring hours 
beyond standard support. Urgent issues are:

- Lost or missing package
- Damaged delivery
- Suspected account fraud
- Package marked delivered but not received
- Payment disputes involving unauthorized charges

To reach after-hours emergency support:

1. Email support@voltnest.com
2. Set the subject line to: **URGENT — [brief issue]**
3. Include your order ID and a description

**Extended monitoring windows**:

| Day | Emergency Hours (EST/EDT) |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM |
| Saturday | 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM |

Urgent emails are answered within **4 business hours** during these 
windows. Issues submitted outside these windows are handled first thing 
the next business day.

## Holiday Schedule

VoltNest support is **closed** on the following US holidays. Emergency 
monitoring does **not** operate on these days unless the issue involves 
suspected fraud.

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | January 1 |
| Martin Luther King Jr. Day | 3rd Monday in January |
| Memorial Day | Last Monday in May |
| Independence Day | July 4 |
| Labor Day | 1st Monday in September |
| Thanksgiving Day | 4th Thursday in November |
| Day After Thanksgiving | 4th Friday in November |
| Christmas Eve | December 24 |
| Christmas Day | December 25 |
| New Year's Eve | December 31 |

If a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, the closure shifts to the 
nearest weekday.

## Peak Season Notice

During the following periods, support response times may be **up to 
48 hours** (instead of 24) due to high volume:

- Black Friday through Cyber Monday (last week of November)
- December 15 – December 26
- The week of July 4
- The week of Thanksgiving

For urgent issues during peak season, always use the **URGENT** subject 
line — those still get priority.

## How to Check If We're Currently Open

The AI assistant on voltnest.com/chat is available 24/7 and can tell you:

- Whether human support is currently online
- The estimated wait time
- When the next human agent will be available

You can also check our live status page at **status.voltnest.com**.
