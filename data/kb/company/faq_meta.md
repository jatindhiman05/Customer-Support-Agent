# Meta FAQ (About Our Support)

> **Category**: company  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

This page answers questions about *how* VoltNest support works — not 
about products or policies, but about the support experience itself. 
Common questions like "Are you a bot?" and "How do I reach a human?" 
are answered here.

## Is the VoltNest assistant a real person?

No. The VoltNest assistant is an **AI-powered virtual agent** trained on 
VoltNest's policies, product catalog, and troubleshooting guides. It can:

- Answer questions about products, shipping, returns, and warranties
- Look up order status if you provide an order ID
- Walk you through common troubleshooting steps
- Escalate to a human agent when needed

It cannot make judgment calls on complex issues, approve exceptions to 
policy, or handle sensitive account matters. For those, it will hand 
you off to a human.

## How do I know if I'm talking to AI or a human?

The AI assistant always identifies itself at the start of a conversation 
with: *"Hi! I'm the VoltNest AI assistant."*

Human agents sign their messages with their first name, e.g., 
*"— Sarah from VoltNest Support"*.

If you're ever unsure, just ask: *"Am I talking to a bot?"* — and you'll 
get a straight answer.

## How do I reach a human?

Three ways, all easy:

1. **In chat**: Type `"talk to a human"`, `"agent"`, or `"representative"` 
   at any point. The AI will hand you off immediately.
2. **By email**: Send to support@voltnest.com. A human reads every email.
3. **By phone**: Call +1 (512) 555-0142 during [support hours](./support_hours.md).

Human agents respond within **24 hours** for standard issues, and within 
**4 business hours** for urgent issues (lost packages, fraud, damage).

## What information should I have ready?

To resolve your issue faster, have the following ready before contacting 
support:

### For order issues:
- Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- Email used at checkout
- Description of the issue

### For product issues:
- Product name or SKU
- Purchase date
- Photos if reporting damage

### For account issues:
- Email linked to your account
- Description of what's happening

Providing this upfront saves an average of **2 back-and-forth emails** 
and gets you a faster resolution.

## Do you keep records of my chats and emails?

Yes. VoltNest stores conversation transcripts (chat and email) 
securely for **24 months**. We use them for:

- Resolving disputes
- Improving support quality
- Training our AI assistant
- Compliance with consumer protection laws

For full details on what we collect and how we use it, see our 
[Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md).

You can request a copy of your conversation history or ask us to delete 
it by emailing privacy@voltnest.com.

## Can I get support in languages other than English?

Currently, VoltNest support is **English-only**. We plan to add Spanish 
and French in **late 2026**. Our AI assistant can attempt basic 
translation for simple questions, but cannot guarantee accuracy.

## Can I get support outside of business hours?

Yes, with limitations:

- **AI assistant**: Available 24/7 for instant answers
- **Email**: You can submit anytime; humans reply during [support hours](./support_hours.md)
- **Urgent issues**: Monitored during [extended emergency hours](./support_hours.md) — mark subject as **URGENT**
- **Live chat & phone**: Only during standard support hours

For most after-hours questions, the AI assistant can help immediately.

## Why did the AI say "I don't know"?

Our AI is designed to **never make things up**. If it can't find an 
answer in VoltNest's knowledge base, it will say so and offer to 
escalate to a human agent — rather than guessing.

This is intentional. A wrong answer is worse than no answer. If the AI 
says "I don't know," you can:

- Rephrase your question
- Ask it to escalate to a human
- Email support@voltnest.com directly

## How do I give feedback about support?

Three ways:

1. **Post-chat survey**: After every chat, you'll see a 1-question 
   rating (👍 / 👎) with optional comments.
2. **Email**: feedback@voltnest.com — read by our support manager daily.
3. **Public reviews**: Leave a review on Trustpilot or Google.

We read every piece of feedback. If you had a bad experience, tell us 
— we'll make it right.

## What if I disagree with a support decision?

If a support agent makes a decision you believe is wrong:

1. Reply to the same email/ticket and ask for a **supervisor review**
2. Your case is escalated to a support manager within 1 business day
3. The manager reviews the full conversation and policy
4. You receive a written decision within 3 business days

We don't always get it right the first time, but we always take a 
second look when asked.
