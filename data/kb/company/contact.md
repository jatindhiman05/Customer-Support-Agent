## Before You Contact Support

To resolve your issue faster, please have the following ready:

### For order-related issues:
- Your **order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`, e.g., `ORD-100001`)
- The **email address** used at checkout
- A brief description of the issue
- Screenshots or photos if applicable

### For product-related issues:
- **Product name or SKU** (e.g., "VoltBuds Pro" or `VBP-2024-BLK`)
- **Purchase date**
- Photos of any damage or defect
- Your order ID (if purchased directly from us)

### For account issues:
- The **email address** linked to your account
- The specific issue (login, password, 2FA, etc.)

Providing this upfront saves an average of **2 back-and-forth emails** 
and speeds resolution significantly.

## Urgent Order Issues

If your issue involves any of the following, mark your email subject 
line as **URGENT**:

- Lost or missing package
- Damaged delivery
- Suspected fraud on your account
- Package marked delivered but not received

**URGENT** emails are prioritized and answered within **4 business hours** 
during the following windows:

- Monday–Friday: 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM EST
- Saturday–Sunday: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM EST

## What We Cannot Help With

To save you time, VoltNest support cannot:

- Provide medical, legal, or financial advice
- Troubleshoot devices not sold by VoltNest
- Handle issues with orders placed on third-party marketplaces
  (Amazon, eBay, etc.) — please contact the seller directly
- Recover lost or stolen products not covered under warranty
