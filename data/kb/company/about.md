# About VoltNest

> **Category**: company  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

VoltNest is an online retailer of premium electronics accessories, 
founded in 2019 and headquartered in Austin, Texas. We design and sell 
audio gear, charging accessories, phone protection, and smart wearables 
for everyday use.

## Our Story

VoltNest was founded in 2019 by two former audio engineers who were 
tired of two things: cheap accessories that broke within months, and 
premium brands that charged $200 for a $40 product.

We started with a single product — a pair of wireless earbuds designed 
to last longer than a phone contract. It sold out in three weeks. Since 
then, we've grown to a curated catalog of six products, all designed 
in-house and tested for at least six months before release.

Our philosophy is simple: **fewer products, better made, supported for 
years.** We don't release 100 new SKUs a year. We release one or two, 
and we make sure they're worth owning.

## What We Sell

VoltNest focuses on a small, curated product line rather than a massive 
catalog. We currently offer six core products across four categories:

- **Audio**: VoltBuds Pro wireless earbuds, VoltBoom Bluetooth speaker
- **Power**: VoltCharge 65W USB-C charger, VoltBank 20K power bank
- **Protection**: VoltShield phone case
- **Wearables**: VoltFit smartwatch band

Every product is designed in-house and tested for at least six months 
before release.

## Where We Operate

VoltNest ships to the **United States** and **12 international markets**, 
including:

- Canada
- United Kingdom
- European Union (all member states)
- Australia
- New Zealand
- India
- Japan
- Singapore

We are headquartered in Austin, Texas, with a distribution center in 
Memphis, Tennessee, and a customer support team distributed across the 
United States.

## Our Promise to You

Every VoltNest product comes with:

- **12-month warranty** covering manufacturing defects
- **Free returns within 30 days** of delivery
- **Free shipping** on US orders over $35
- **Real human support** within 24 hours for any issue
- **No restocking fees** — ever

We stand behind what we sell. If a product fails within warranty, we 
repair or replace it at no cost to you.

## How We're Different

Most electronics accessory brands chase volume. We don't. We release 
1–2 new products per year, refine them obsessively, and support them 
for years after purchase. Our goal is not to sell you more — it's to 
make sure the thing you bought works perfectly.

## Sustainability

We believe tech accessories shouldn't cost the planet. Our commitments:

- **100% recyclable packaging** — no plastic windows, no foam
- **Carbon-offset shipping** through our carrier partners
- **Repair-first warranty** — we fix before we replace when possible
- **Defective returns** are refurbished and donated, not landfilled
- **Battery recycling** program for all power products (returns 
  prepaid)
