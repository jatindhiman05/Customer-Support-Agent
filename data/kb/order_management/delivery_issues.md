# Delivery Issues (Troubleshooting)

> **Category**: order_management  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Diagnostic guide for problems with deliveries — packages marked 
delivered but not received, lost in transit, stuck in customs, damaged 
on arrival, returned to sender, or delivered to the wrong address. 
Follow the relevant section for your specific issue.

## Overview

Delivery problems fall into **7 categories**. Find yours below:

| Issue | Section |
|---|---|
| Marked "Delivered" but not received | A |
| Lost in transit (no tracking update) | B |
| Stuck in customs (international) | C |
| Damaged on arrival | D |
| Delivered to wrong address | E |
| Returned to sender | F |
| Delivery attempted, no one home | G |

**Related**: [Track Shipment Guide](./track_shipment_guide.md) — for 
how to read carrier tracking statuses.

## Before You Start

Quick facts:

- **Carriers sometimes mark "Delivered" up to 24 hours early.** Wait 
  before assuming loss.
- **Every carrier has a claims process.** We file on your behalf.
- **Time is critical.** The longer you wait, the harder recovery is.
- **Damage must be reported within 48 hours** of delivery for shipping 
  claims.
- **Lost packages are declared after 10 business days** (US) or **21 
  business days** (international) with no tracking updates.

## Section A: Package Marked "Delivered" But Not Received

This is the **#1 delivery issue**. Before assuming theft or loss, 
check these.

### Step A1: Wait 24 hours
Carriers sometimes mark packages "Delivered" hours before actual 
delivery. Wait **24 hours** and re-check.

### Step A2: Check common hiding spots
Drivers often leave packages in specific places:

- Behind bushes or planters
- Behind pillars or porch columns
- Under doormats or welcome mats
- At the side or back door
- In a garage or carport
- With the front desk or building manager

### Step A3: Ask neighbors
Packages are frequently delivered to neighbors by mistake — especially 
in apartment buildings or on shared driveways. Politely ask immediate 
neighbors.

### Step A4: Check with your building
If you live in an apartment:

- Ask the front desk or leasing office
- Check the mailroom
- Check any package locker system (Amazon Hub, Luxer, etc.)

### Step A5: Check the carrier's photo
Most carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL) provide a **delivery photo** 
when they mark a package delivered. Open your tracking page and look 
for "View delivery photo."

This shows exactly where the driver left the package.

### Step A6: Contact the carrier
If you can't find the package after 24 hours, contact the carrier 
directly:

1. Call the carrier's customer service
2. Provide your tracking number
3. Request a **delivery investigation**
4. Get a case number

The carrier can:

- Confirm the exact delivery location
- Ask the driver
- Attempt recovery if misdelivered
- Provide documentation for a claim

### Step A7: If still missing, contact VoltNest
After the carrier investigation, if the package is confirmed lost or 
misdelivered to an unrecoverable address:

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Package Marked 
   Delivered — Not Received"**
2. Include:
   - Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
   - Tracking number
   - Carrier case number
   - Description of what you found (or didn't find)
3. We'll file a **carrier claim** on your behalf

**Resolution**: Most cases are resolved with a **full refund or free 
replacement** within **10 business days** of the investigation opening.

## Section B: Lost in Transit (No Tracking Update)

A package is considered **lost** if:

- **Domestic**: no tracking update for **7+ business days**
- **International**: no tracking update for **14+ business days**

### Step B1: Check for stalled tracking
Tracking can pause for 1–3 days normally. Only flag concern after:

- **7 days** (US domestic)
- **14 days** (international)

### Step B2: Check tracking on the carrier's site
Sometimes the VoltNest page lags. Check directly on the carrier's 
website with your tracking number.

### Step B3: Contact the carrier
After the threshold:

1. Call the carrier with your tracking number
2. Request a **trace** (search for the package)
3. Get a case number

Carrier traces usually take **2–5 business days**.

### Step B4: Contact VoltNest
If the carrier can't locate the package within the trace window:

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Package Lost in Transit"**
2. Include:
   - Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
   - Tracking number
   - Carrier case number
   - Date of last tracking update

3. We'll open a formal investigation with the carrier

**Resolution timeline**: 
- Investigation opens within **4 business hours**
- Resolution in **10 business days** from investigation start

**What you get**:
- **Full refund**, or
- **Free replacement** — your choice

For products that can't be re-shipped internationally (e.g., VoltBank 
20K), refund is the only option.

## Section C: Stuck in Customs (International)

Customs holds are common for international orders and usually resolve 
without intervention.

### Step C1: Understand the timeline
Typical customs timelines:

| Region | Typical Hold |
|---|---|
| Canada | 1–3 business days |
| UK / EU | 3–7 business days |
| Australia / NZ | 2–5 business days |
| India | 5–10 business days |
| Japan / Singapore | 2–5 business days |

**Delays are not unusual.** Peak seasons (holidays) can double these 
times.

### Step C2: Check if customs needs anything from you
Sometimes customs holds a package because they need:

- **Payment of duties/taxes** — check your email and tracking for 
  instructions
- **Additional documentation** — some countries require import 
  declaration
- **Identity verification** — some countries ask the recipient to 
  verify

If the carrier or customs has requested something, **respond promptly** 
— unanswered requests can lead to the package being returned.

### Step C3: Contact your local customs office
After the typical hold time, contact your country's customs office 
with your tracking number. They can:

- Confirm the package is in their system
- Explain what's holding it
- Tell you what's needed

### Step C4: Contact the carrier's international desk
If customs says the package cleared but tracking hasn't updated, 
contact the international carrier (often the local postal service):

- Royal Mail (UK)
- Canada Post (Canada)
- Australia Post (AU)
- India Post (India)

### Step C5: Contact VoltNest if still stuck
If the package is stuck in customs for **more than 21 business days** 
with no updates, contact support@voltnest.com with subject **"Stuck 
in Customs"**. We'll:

1. Open a carrier investigation
2. Determine if the package is recoverable
3. Issue a refund if confirmed lost

**Note**: Customs-seized packages may not be recoverable. In that 
case, refund is issued after the carrier confirms the loss.

### If duties aren't paid
If you refuse to pay duties and the package is returned:

- Shipping fees are **not refunded**
- A **15% restocking fee** may apply
- Item cost is refunded once we receive the package

## Section D: Damaged on Arrival

If your package arrives with visible damage or the product is broken:

### Step D1: Report within 48 hours
Damage claims must be filed within **48 hours** of delivery. After 
that, shipping insurance may not cover the claim.

### Step D2: Document the damage
Before opening or fully unpacking:

1. **Photograph the package exterior** — every side, any visible 
   damage
2. **Photograph the shipping label** — shows tracking number
3. **Open carefully** — preserve all packaging
4. **Photograph the product** — the damage itself
5. **Photograph all packaging materials** — bubble wrap, foam, etc.

**Do not discard any packaging.** Carriers require it for claims.

### Step D3: Contact VoltNest
Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Damaged on Arrival"** and 
include:

- Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- Photos (all 5 above)
- Description of the damage
- Whether the product is functional or unusable

### Step D4: What we do
1. Response within **24 hours**
2. We file a claim with the carrier
3. **We ship a replacement immediately** (before receiving the 
   damaged item) for verified damage
4. You receive a prepaid label to return the damaged item
5. No cost to you

**Important**: Do not attempt to use a damaged electrical product 
(charger, power bank, etc.). Safety risk. Contact us immediately.

**Related**: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md)

## Section E: Delivered to Wrong Address

If tracking shows delivered but the photo shows a different location 
or the carrier confirms misdelivery:

### Step E1: Contact the carrier immediately
Time-critical. The carrier may be able to recover the package:

1. Call the carrier's customer service
2. Provide tracking number
3. Explain the misdelivery
4. Request a recovery attempt

### Step E2: If the package is at a neighbor's
If the delivery photo shows a neighbor's house, politely ask them. 
Most people return misdelivered packages.

### Step E3: If unrecoverable, contact VoltNest
Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Misdelivered Package"** 
and include:

- Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- Tracking number
- Carrier case number
- Delivery photo (if available)

We'll file a carrier claim. If the carrier confirms misdelivery:
- **Refund or replacement** — your choice
- Resolution in **10 business days**

## Section F: Returned to Sender

If tracking shows "Returned to Sender," the package is on its way 
back to our Memphis warehouse.

### Common reasons for return
- Multiple failed delivery attempts
- Address undeliverable (invalid or incomplete)
- Refused delivery by customer
- Customs refused (international)
- Signature required, no one home

### What happens next
1. Package arrives at our warehouse in **3–7 business days**
2. Our team inspects and processes the return
3. Refund is issued within **5–7 business days** of arrival
4. You receive an email confirmation

### Refund calculation
- **VoltNest error**: full refund including shipping
- **Customer error (wrong address)**: refund minus shipping
- **Refused delivery**: refund minus shipping, may include 15% 
  restocking fee (international)
- **Customs refused**: refund minus shipping, minus any customs 
  fees paid by VoltNest

## Section G: Delivery Attempted, No One Home

If tracking shows "Delivery Attempted," the carrier tried to deliver 
but couldn't.

### Common reasons
- Signature required, no one home
- Business closed (delivery during off hours)
- Address inaccessible (gate code, building access)
- Package too large for mailbox

### What to do
1. Check the carrier's tracking page for **redelivery instructions**
2. Contact the carrier to **reschedule delivery**
3. Or request **pickup at a local facility**
4. Some carriers auto-redeliver the next business day

### If you miss multiple attempts
After **2–3 failed attempts**, the carrier returns the package to 
sender. See Section F.

### Preventing failed deliveries
- Add delivery instructions via the carrier's app (UPS My Choice, 
  USPS Informed Delivery, FedEx Delivery Manager)
- Provide a phone number for delivery notifications
- Use a work address for high-value orders
- Sign up for text alerts

## Section H: Package Shows "Exception" or "Delay"

Exceptions are temporary disruptions:

- Weather (storms, snow, floods)
- Mechanical issues at carrier facility
- Local disruptions (road closures, etc.)
- Customs (international)

### What to do
1. Check the carrier's tracking page for details
2. Wait **5 business days** — most exceptions resolve
3. If no update after 5 days, contact the carrier
4. If still unresolved, contact VoltNest

### Do we refund for delays?
No — carrier delays are outside our control. But we will:

- Provide updated ETAs when available
- Offer a **10% discount on your next order** if delay exceeds 
  **5 business days beyond the original estimate**

## Section I: Signature Required — Not Home

Some high-value orders require a signature.

### What "signature required" means
- The carrier will not leave the package without a signature
- Someone 18+ must be present to sign
- ID may be required

### Options
1. **Be home**: Track the package and be available on delivery day
2. **Reschedule**: Request a specific delivery window via the 
   carrier's app or website
3. **Redirect**: Send to a pickup point (UPS Store, FedEx Office, 
   etc.) via the carrier's app
4. **Authorize release**: Some carriers let you sign a release 
   waiver online

**Sign up for carrier apps** (all free):
- UPS My Choice
- USPS Informed Delivery
- FedEx Delivery Manager
- DHL Express (for international)

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- Package marked "Delivered" but not received after **24 hours** and 
  carrier investigation
- Package lost in transit after **7 days (US) / 14 days (intl)** with 
  no tracking update
- Package stuck in customs for **more than 21 business days**
- Package arrived damaged (within **48 hours** of delivery)
- Package delivered to wrong address and carrier confirms misdelivery
- Package returned to sender but refund not issued within **10 business 
  days** of arrival at our warehouse
- Any delivery issue that the carrier can't resolve

### What to Include

1. **Order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
2. **Tracking number**
3. **Carrier**
4. **Current tracking status**
5. **Description of the issue**
6. **Carrier case number** (if you have one)
7. **Photos** (for damage claims)
8. **Steps you've already taken**

### How to Contact

- **Email**: support@voltnest.com
- **Live chat**: voltnest.com/chat
- **Subject lines**: use the exact subject from each section for 
  fastest routing

### Response Time

- **Package marked delivered but missing (URGENT)**: 4 business hours
- **Lost in transit**: 24 hours
- **Damage claim**: 24 hours
- **Other delivery issues**: 24 hours

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 and Tier 2 agents handling delivery issues.

### Triage Questions
- What does tracking currently show?
- When was the last tracking update?
- Have you contacted the carrier directly? Do you have a case number?
- Have you checked with neighbors / building / common hiding spots?
- Is this international? Is it held in customs?
- For damage: do you have photos? Were they taken before opening?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Guiding customer through carrier investigation
- Explaining tracking statuses
- Confirming damage claim requirements
- Advising on signature-required deliveries
- Documenting carrier case numbers

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Package marked delivered but missing after 24 hours + carrier 
  investigation
- Package confirmed lost by carrier
- Carrier confirms misdelivery
- Package stuck in customs > 21 business days
- Damage claim approved
- Multiple delivery issues on same customer account

### Resolution Authority

| Issue | Tier 1 Authority | Tier 2 Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Reship/replace | ❌ | ✅ Up to $200 |
| Refund | ❌ | ✅ Full order value |
| Goodwill credit | Up to $10 | Up to $50 |
| Open carrier investigation | ❌ | ✅ |

### Carrier Investigation Protocol
For Tier 2, open investigation with the carrier:

1. **Gather**: order ID, tracking, ship date, value, contents
2. **File**: through carrier's business portal (not consumer site)
3. **Interim update**: to customer within 4 business hours
4. **Follow-up**: every 48 hours until resolved
5. **SLA**: resolution within **10 business days**

### Lost Package Resolution
Once carrier confirms lost:

1. Offer customer **refund OR replacement** — their choice
2. For international products that can't be reshipped (VoltBank 20K), 
   refund only
3. Process within **10 business days** of confirmation
4. Document in CRM

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not promise a "Delivered" package can be recovered — many can't
- Do not blame the customer for missing packages without evidence
- Do not tell them we can "make the carrier deliver faster"
- Do not offer compensation beyond tier authority
- Do not close a delivery case until the customer confirms resolution
- Do not promise refund timelines shorter than our standards

### Peak Season Adjustments
During peak seasons (Black Friday, Dec 15–26, July 4 week, 
Thanksgiving week):

- Extend all "no update" thresholds by **3 business days**
- Extend damage report window from 48 hours to **72 hours**
- Prioritize package-not-received cases as URGENT
