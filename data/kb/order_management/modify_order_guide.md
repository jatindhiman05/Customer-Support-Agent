# Modify Order Guide

> **Category**: order_management  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Step-by-step guide for modifying a VoltNest order after placing it — 
changing your shipping address, adding or removing items, upgrading 
shipping, adding a gift message, or updating contact info. Includes 
what's possible at each order status.

## Overview

**What you can modify depends on your order status.** Once an order 
is **Packed**, most modifications are no longer possible. Once 
**Shipped**, only carrier-side changes (like intercepts) are available.

| Order Status | Address | Items | Shipping | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payment Pending | ⏸ Wait | ⏸ Wait | ⏸ Wait | Wait for Confirmed |
| Confirmed | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Self-service |
| Processing | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Self-service |
| Packed | ⚠️ Support | ❌ No | ⚠️ Support | Very limited |
| Shipped | ⚠️ Intercept ($15) | ❌ No | ❌ No | Carrier-only |
| In Transit | ⚠️ Intercept ($15) | ❌ No | ❌ No | Carrier-only |
| Out for Delivery | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | Too late |
| Delivered | ❌ No | ❌ No | ❌ No | Return instead |

**Related**: [Order Status Guide](./order_status_guide.md) — for what 
each status means.

## Before You Start

Quick checks:

1. **Find your current order status** in Account → Orders
2. **Identify what you want to change** — address, items, shipping, 
   or contact info
3. **Have your order ID ready** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
4. **Check if you have a shipping confirmation email** — if yes, the 
   order is Shipped (Section F applies)

## Section A: Change Shipping Address

### A1. Before Packing (Self-Service)

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

**Steps**:
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click on the order (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
4. Click **"Edit Shipping Address"**
5. Enter the new address
6. Confirm the change

You'll receive a confirmation email. The order resumes processing 
immediately.

**Important**: Address changes do not affect orders that have already 
shipped. If your order shows "Shipped," use A3 (carrier intercept).

### A2. While Packed (Support Only)

**Available in**: Packed status.

Once packed, the label is printed. The address on the label **cannot 
be changed** — but support may be able to reprint the label if the 
carrier hasn't picked up yet.

**Steps**:
1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Address Change — Urgent"**
2. Include:
   - Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
   - Correct shipping address
   - Time of your request
3. Support attempts to update the label before carrier pickup
4. Response within **4 business hours**

**Note**: If the carrier picks up before support can act, use A3.

### A3. After Shipping (Carrier Intercept — $15)

**Available in**: Shipped, In Transit status.

Once shipped, we can't change the address on the label. But we can 
request a **carrier intercept** to redirect the package to a new 
address.

**Cost**: **$15 USD** — this is the carrier's fee, passed to you.

**Requirements**:
- Order is still in transit (not delivered)
- New address is within the same country
- Carrier supports intercepts (UPS, FedEx, USPS do; some don't)
- Customer accepts the $15 fee

**Steps**:
1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Carrier Intercept Request"**
2. Include:
   - Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
   - New shipping address
   - Confirmation you accept the $15 fee
3. Support files the intercept with the carrier
4. You'll be notified within **4 business hours** if the intercept 
   succeeds

**Success rate**: Approximately **70%** if the request is made early 
in transit. Lower as delivery approaches.

**If the intercept fails**:
- The package will be delivered to the original address
- You can attempt to retrieve it from the recipient, or
- File a claim with the carrier for misdelivery

**Refund of the $15 fee**: Only if the intercept fails for a reason 
attributable to VoltNest or the carrier.

### A4. Address Changes Not Supported

- **Cross-country intercepts** — not available
- **Delivered packages** — no changes possible
- **Signature required on new address** — cannot be added after shipping

## Section B: Add Items to an Order

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

You can add items to an existing order before it's packed.

### Steps
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click the order (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
4. Click **"Add Items"**
5. Browse and select additional items
6. Confirm the addition
7. Pay the difference at checkout

**What happens**:
- New items ship together with your original order
- Shipping cost recalculated if total changes
- You'll receive an updated confirmation email

**Free shipping note**: If adding items pushes your total **above 
$35**, free shipping applies automatically.

### If the order is already Packed
You **cannot** add items. Place a separate order for the additional 
items. Your original order will ship as-is.

## Section C: Remove Items from an Order

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

You can remove individual items before the order is packed. This is 
called a **partial cancellation**.

### Steps
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click the order
4. Click **"Modify Order"**
5. Uncheck the items to remove
6. Confirm the modification

**What happens**:
- Removed items are refunded within **1 business day**
- Remaining items continue processing
- Shipping cost recalculated based on the new total

**Shipping recalculation**:
- **Above $35**: Free shipping still applies
- **Below $35**: Standard shipping ($4.99) is added
- **Paid for Express/Overnight**: Shipping method unchanged

**Refund**: Issued to the original payment method. See 
[Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md) for 
timelines.

**Related**: [Cancel Order Guide](./cancel_order_guide.md) — Section E 
covers partial cancellations in more detail.

### If the order is already Packed
Partial cancellations are no longer possible. You must accept delivery 
and return the unwanted items.

## Section D: Change Shipping Method

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

You can upgrade or downgrade your shipping method before packing.

### Steps
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click the order
4. Click **"Change Shipping"**
5. Choose a new shipping method:
   - Standard (5–7 days) — $4.99 (free over $35)
   - Express (2–3 days) — $12.99
   - Overnight (next business day) — $24.99
6. Confirm the change

**Cost adjustment**:
- **Upgrading**: You pay the difference
- **Downgrading**: Refunded the difference
- **No change fee** — the shipping cost is recalculated automatically

**Important cutoff**: Overnight shipping is only available if ordered 
before **2:00 PM EST**. After that, the option won't be available.

**Peak season**: During Black Friday, December 15–26, the week of 
July 4, and Thanksgiving week, express and overnight availability may 
be limited.

### If the order is already Packed
Shipping method cannot be changed once the label is printed. Contact 
support if the order is Packed but not yet picked up — occasionally 
we can still modify if the carrier hasn't scanned it.

## Section E: Change Order Contact Information

### E1. Change the email address on an order

**Not possible.** The email address on an order is locked once the 
order is placed. You can:

- Update the email on your **account** for future orders
- Ask support to forward order notifications to a new email (one-time, 
  for this specific order)

### E2. Change the phone number for delivery

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

The phone number is used for carrier delivery notifications.

**Steps**:
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click the order
4. Click **"Edit Contact Info"**
5. Update the phone number

Once shipped, only the carrier can update the delivery phone — do 
this through the carrier's app or website.

### E3. Add delivery instructions

**Not supported through VoltNest.** Delivery instructions (leave at 
door, knock, etc.) can be added directly through the carrier's app 
or website after the package ships.

**Sign up for the carrier's app**:
- UPS My Choice (free)
- USPS Informed Delivery (free)
- FedEx Delivery Manager (free)

## Section F: Add a Gift Message

**Available in**: Confirmed, Processing status.

If your order is a gift, you can add a gift message.

### Steps
1. Log in to voltnest.com
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Click the order
4. Click **"Add Gift Message"**
5. Enter your message (up to **250 characters**)
6. Confirm

**What happens**:
- Message is printed on a gift card and included in the package
- The packing slip shows the gift message (no price shown)
- Gift recipient doesn't see pricing

**Cost**: **Free**.

### If the order has already shipped
Gift messages cannot be added after shipping. You can:

- Send a separate gift card (see [Promo & Gift Card FAQ](../faqs/promo_giftcard_faq.md))
- Send a handwritten note to the recipient

## Section G: Change Payment Method

**Not possible after placing an order.**

Once an order is confirmed, the payment method is locked. You cannot:

- Switch from one card to another
- Change from card to PayPal
- Change from PayPal to Klarna

**Why**: The payment has already been authorized. Changing methods 
requires cancelling and reordering.

**If you need to change payment**:
1. Cancel the order (if not shipped)
2. Place a new order with the desired payment method
3. See [Cancel Order Guide](./cancel_order_guide.md)

**Related**: [Payment FAQ](../faqs/payment_faq.md)

## Section H: What Cannot Be Modified

Regardless of status, these cannot be changed after ordering:

- **Email address on the order** (only future orders)
- **Payment method** (must cancel and reorder)
- **Applied promo code** (see [Promo & Gift Card FAQ](../faqs/promo_giftcard_faq.md))
- **Redeemed loyalty points** (returned to balance if cancelled)
- **Product customization** (engraving, personalization)
- **Order date or order ID**
- **Applied gift card balance**

## Section I: Support-Only Modifications

Some changes require support even at early statuses:

- **Split shipment**: Ship items to different addresses (requires 
  separate orders — support can help)
- **Overnight upgrade after 2 PM EST**: Not possible automatically
- **International intercept**: Only if within the same country
- **Bulk order changes**: For 10+ items
- **Corporate order**: Custom invoice or PO number changes

Contact support@voltnest.com with your request and order ID. Response 
within **24 hours**.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- Self-service modification is unavailable but the order hasn't shipped
- You need a carrier intercept ($15) for a shipped order
- You need a bulk order modification
- You're in peak season and need urgent modifications
- The self-service option fails
- You need to modify an order that's in an unusual state

### What to Include

1. **Order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
2. **Current order status**
3. **What you want to change**
4. **New value** (new address, new items, etc.)
5. **Reason** (optional but helpful)

### How to Contact

- **Email**: support@voltnest.com, subject "Modify Order"
- **Live chat**: voltnest.com/chat
- **Urgent address change**: subject "Address Change — Urgent"

### Response Time

- **Urgent address change (while Packed)**: 4 business hours
- **Carrier intercept**: 4 business hours
- **Standard modification**: 24 hours

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 agents handling modification requests.

### Triage Questions
- What is the current order status?
- What do you want to change?
- When was the order placed?
- Has the order shipped? (check for tracking number)
- Is this a peak season window?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Guiding self-service address change
- Guiding add items / remove items
- Guiding shipping method change
- Adding gift messages (if not shipped)
- Explaining what's not possible

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Carrier intercept requested ($15)
- Address change while Packed (label reprint may be needed)
- Bulk modifications (10+ items)
- Split shipment requests
- Self-service fails unexpectedly

### Modification Rules by Status

| Status | Address | Items | Shipping | Gift Msg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed | Self | Self | Self | Self |
| Processing | Self | Self | Self | Self |
| Packed | Support | ❌ | Support | ❌ |
| Shipped | Intercept ($15) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Delivered | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not promise a carrier intercept will succeed
- Do not promise shipping method changes once Packed
- Do not share the customer's payment method change options as 
  "possible" — it requires cancel/reorder
- Do not commit to timeframes outside our SLAs
- Do not tell them we can add a gift message after shipping

### Peak Season Modifications
During peak seasons (Black Friday, Dec 15–26, July 4 week, 
Thanksgiving week), the modification window closes faster. Treat 
address change requests as **urgent** even without the flag.
