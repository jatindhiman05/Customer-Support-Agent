# Cancel Order Guide

> **Category**: order_management  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Step-by-step guide for cancelling a VoltNest order — before shipping, 
during packing, or after the carrier has picked it up. Includes refund 
timelines, partial cancellations, and what to do if cancellation isn't 
possible.

## Overview

**Can you cancel?** Depends on the order's current status:

| Order Status | Can Cancel? | How |
|---|---|---|
| Payment Pending | ✅ Yes | Wait 10 min, then cancel |
| Confirmed | ✅ Yes | Self-service |
| Processing | ✅ Yes | Self-service |
| Packed | ⚠️ Support only | Contact support (intercept) |
| Shipped | ❌ No | Must return after delivery |
| In Transit | ❌ No | Must return after delivery |
| Out for Delivery | ❌ No | Refuse delivery or return |
| Delivered | ❌ No | Return process applies |

**Self-service cancellation** is available while the order is in 
**Confirmed** or **Processing** status. Once it reaches **Packed**, 
you'll need to contact support.

**Related**: [Order Status Guide](./order_status_guide.md) — for what 
each status means.

## Before You Start

Quick checks:

1. **Is the order already shipped?** Check your email for a shipping 
   confirmation. If you have one, the order is Shipped — proceed to 
   Section D.
2. **Did you use a promo code or gift card?** See "Refunds for 
   discounted orders" below.
3. **Is it a multi-item order?** You may want a partial cancellation 
   (Section E).
4. **Are you cancelling because of a payment failure?** See 
   [Payment Failures](../troubleshooting/payment_failures.md) instead.

## Section A: Cancel Before Packing (Self-Service)

This is the easiest path — available until the order reaches "Packed" 
status.

### Steps

1. **Log in** to your VoltNest account at voltnest.com/login
2. Go to **Account → Orders**
3. Find the order you want to cancel (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
4. Click on the order to open its detail page
5. Click the **"Cancel Order"** button (usually on the right side)
6. Select a reason from the dropdown:
   - Changed my mind
   - Ordered by mistake
   - Found a better price
   - Shipping is too slow
   - Other (add a note)
7. Click **"Confirm Cancellation"**

You'll receive a confirmation email within a few minutes.

### What happens next

- Order status changes to **"Cancelled"**
- Refund is processed within **1 business day**
- Refund arrives in your account within **5–7 business days** 
  (card) or **3–5 business days** (PayPal)
- You'll receive a refund confirmation email

### Common issues

**"Cancel Order" button is greyed out**
The order is already Packed or later. Proceed to Section B.

**"Cancel Order" button doesn't appear**
The order may already be Shipped. Check the order status. Proceed to 
Section D.

**Multiple attempts fail**
Try in a different browser or device. If it still fails, contact 
support.

## Section B: Cancel While Packed (Support Only)

Once an order is **Packed**, self-service cancellation is disabled. 
You must contact support to attempt a cancellation before the carrier 
picks it up.

**Window of opportunity**: Usually **1–4 hours** between Packed and 
Shipped. Act quickly.

### Steps

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Urgent Cancellation"**
2. Include:
   - Order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
   - Reason for cancellation
   - Your request: "Please cancel before carrier pickup"
3. Check your email frequently — we respond within **4 business hours** 
   for urgent cancellations

**Note**: Submitting a cancellation request does **not guarantee** 
cancellation. If the carrier picks up the package before support can 
act, the order will ship, and you'll need to return it (Section D).

### What happens next

- If cancelled successfully → refund in **2 business days**
- If carrier picked up first → order ships; use return process

## Section C: Cancel After Shipping (Carrier Intercept)

If the order is already **Shipped**, self-cancel and support-cancel 
are no longer available. You have two options:

### Option 1: Carrier Intercept (Fast, Fee Applies)

We can request the carrier to intercept the package and return it. 
This costs **$15** and is not guaranteed.

**Requirements**:
- Order is still in transit (not delivered)
- Carrier supports intercept (most do)
- Customer accepts the $15 fee

### Steps

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Carrier Intercept Request"**
2. Include your order ID and confirmation that you accept the $15 fee
3. Support files the intercept request with the carrier
4. If successful, the package is returned to us
5. Refund is processed **5–7 business days** after arrival at our 
   warehouse

**Success rate**: Approximately **70%** if the request is made early 
in transit. Lower as delivery approaches.

### Option 2: Refuse Delivery

If you know when the carrier will deliver, you can **refuse the 
delivery**. The package returns to us automatically.

**How to refuse**:
- Tell the driver "I refuse this delivery"
- Or leave a signed note on your door
- Do not accept the package

**Costs**:
- Shipping fees are **not refunded** for refused deliveries
- A **15% restocking fee** may apply for refused international deliveries
- Refunds are issued **5–7 business days** after the package arrives 
  back at our warehouse

**Related**: [Delivery Issues](./delivery_issues.md)

### Option 3: Accept and Return

If intercept and refuse aren't viable, accept the package and use the 
standard return process.

- **Return window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Shipping cost**: $6.99 (US), or actual cost (international) unless 
  the return is our error
- **Refund timeline**: 10–17 business days (see 
  [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md))

## Section D: Cancel While In Transit or Delivered

Once the order is **In Transit** or **Delivered**, cancellation is no 
longer possible. You must complete a return.

### Return Process

1. Log in and go to **Account → Orders**
2. Click the delivered order
3. Click **"Return or Exchange"**
4. Select a reason and confirm
5. Print the return label
6. Ship the package within **14 days** of RMA issuance

See the [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md) 
for the full process, conditions, and exceptions.

## Section E: Partial Cancellation (Multi-Item Orders)

If your order has multiple items and you want to cancel only some of 
them, use a partial cancellation.

**Availability**: Partial cancellation works only while the order is 
**Confirmed** or **Processing**. Once Packed, the whole order is 
locked.

### Steps

1. Log in and go to **Account → Orders**
2. Click the order
3. Click **"Modify Order"**
4. Uncheck the items you want to remove
5. Confirm the modification

### What happens to shipping?

- **If the new total is above $35**: Free shipping still applies
- **If the new total drops below $35**: Standard shipping ($4.99) is 
  added
- **If you paid for Express/Overnight**: The shipping cost is **not** 
  recalculated — you keep the fast shipping

**Note**: Shipping fees are non-refundable if you downgrade your 
order total below the free shipping threshold.

### Refund for removed items

- Refund issued within **1 business day** of the partial cancellation
- Refund arrives in **5–7 business days** (card), **3–5 business days** 
  (PayPal)

## Section F: Canceled by VoltNest

Rarely, VoltNest may cancel your order on our end. You'll receive an 
email explaining the reason.

### Common reasons

- **Out of stock** — item sold out between order and packing
- **Pricing error** — order placed at an incorrect price
- **Suspected fraud** — flagged by our payment processor
- **Shipping restriction** — destination not supported
- **Address issue** — carrier flagged the address as invalid

### What we do

1. Send you an email within **24 hours** with the reason
2. Issue a **full refund** within **1 business day**
3. For out-of-stock cancellations, provide a **10% discount code** 
   for your next order

### If you think the cancellation was an error

Reply to the cancellation email with details. Our team reviews 
within **1 business day**. If we made a mistake, we'll reprocess 
the order at the original price or issue an additional goodwill 
credit.

## Section G: Refunds for Discounted Orders

If your order used a **promo code**, **loyalty reward**, or 
**gift card**, the refund calculation is different.

### Promo code orders

- Refund is calculated on **what you paid**, not the original price
- Example: $100 item with $20 promo → you paid $80 → refund is $80
- The promo discount is **not refunded in cash**

### Loyalty reward orders

- Points redeemed are **returned to your loyalty balance**
- The discount applied is not refunded as cash
- See [Loyalty FAQ](../faqs/loyalty_faq.md) for details

### Gift card orders

- Refund is issued as a **new gift card** (emailed within 1 business day)
- You cannot receive a cash refund for a gift-card purchase
- See [Promo & Gift Card FAQ](../faqs/promo_giftcard_faq.md) for details

### Mixed payment orders

If you paid partially with a gift card and partially with a card:

- **Gift card portion** → refunded as a new gift card
- **Card portion** → refunded to the original card
- Refunds are split **proportionally** to the original payment

## Section H: What Cannot Be Cancelled

Some orders cannot be cancelled once placed, regardless of status:

- **Personalized or custom-engraved items** — production begins 
  immediately
- **Pre-orders** — see Pre-Order section below
- **Gift cards** — non-refundable once issued
- **Digital downloads** — non-refundable once delivered
- **Orders that have shipped** — must return after delivery

### Pre-orders

Pre-orders (products not yet released) can be cancelled:

- **Any time before the release date** — full refund
- **On release date, before 2:00 PM EST** — full refund
- **After release date ships** — becomes a standard return

Pre-order cancellations are always free and instant.

## Section I: Refund Timelines Summary

| Cancellation Stage | Refund Issued | Refund Received |
|---|---|---|
| Before Packing | 1 business day | 5–7 days (card), 3–5 days (PayPal) |
| After Packing (support) | 2 business days | 5–7 days |
| Carrier Intercept | 5–7 days after arrival | 5–7 days |
| Refused Delivery | 5–7 days after arrival | 5–7 days |
| Partial Cancellation | 1 business day | 5–7 days |

**Total time from cancellation to refund received: 6–14 business days.**

## Section J: Peak Season Cancellation

During high-volume periods, the self-service cancellation window may 
close **faster than usual** because orders move to packing quickly.

**Peak season periods**:
- Black Friday through Cyber Monday
- December 15 – December 26
- The week of July 4
- The week of Thanksgiving

**During peak season**, the cancellation window may close in 
**under 30 minutes**. If you need to cancel, do it **immediately** 
after placing the order.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- Self-service cancellation is unavailable but the order hasn't shipped
- You want a carrier intercept ($15)
- Your order was cancelled by VoltNest and you disagree
- You need to cancel a multi-item order but can't
- A cancelled order's refund hasn't arrived after 10 business days
- You're in peak season and need urgent cancellation

### What to Include

1. **Order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
2. **Current order status**
3. **Reason** for cancellation
4. **Which refund method** you used (card, PayPal, gift card, etc.)
5. **Timestamp** of when you placed the order (if relevant for peak 
   season)

### How to Contact

- **Email**: support@voltnest.com, subject "Cancellation Request"
- **Live chat**: voltnest.com/chat
- **Urgent cancellations**: mark subject "Urgent Cancellation"

### Response Time

- **Urgent cancellation**: 4 business hours
- **Standard cancellation request**: 24 hours
- **Post-cancellation refund issue**: 24 hours

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 agents handling cancellation requests.

### Triage Questions
- What is the current order status?
- How long ago was the order placed?
- What's the reason for cancellation?
- Did the customer use a promo, loyalty reward, or gift card?
- Is this a partial or full cancellation?
- Are we in a peak season window?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Self-service cancellation guidance
- Refund timeline explanations
- Partial cancellation guidance
- Discount-order refund calculation
- Peak season awareness

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Carrier intercept request ($15)
- VoltNest-initiated cancellation dispute
- Refund not received after 10 business days
- Peak season urgent cancellation (SLA-tight)
- Multi-item partial cancel that fails in system

### Refund Calculation Rules
- **Promo code**: Refund = amount paid, not original price
- **Loyalty points**: Points returned to loyalty balance
- **Gift card**: New gift card issued (1 business day)
- **Mixed payment**: Split proportionally

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not promise cancellation success after Packed status
- Do not promise intercept success — it's ~70% at best
- Do not confirm a refund timeline shorter than the standard
- Do not tell them "just refuse delivery" without explaining the 
  consequences (no shipping refund, potential 15% restocking fee)

### Peak Season Response
During peak season (Black Friday, Dec 15–26, July 4 week, Thanksgiving 
week), treat **all** cancellation requests as **urgent** — even without 
the "Urgent" flag. The window is tight.
