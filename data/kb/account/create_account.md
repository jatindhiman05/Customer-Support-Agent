# Create Account Guide

> **Category**: account  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Step-by-step guide for creating a VoltNest account — from sign-up to 
first login. Covers required information, email verification, password 
rules, and how to fix common signup problems.

## Overview

Creating a VoltNest account takes about **2 minutes**. You'll need:

- An email address
- A password (rules below)
- (Optional but recommended) Your shipping address

An account is **required** to place orders. Guest checkout is not 
available at VoltNest.

## Why Create an Account?

An account lets you:

- Place and track orders
- Manage returns and warranty claims
- Earn loyalty points (see [Loyalty FAQ](../faqs/loyalty_faq.md))
- Save addresses and payment methods
- View order history

Without an account, you cannot shop with VoltNest.

## Before You Start

Quick checks:

- **Use a permanent email** — order confirmations, shipping updates, 
  and warranty notices all go to this address
- **Don't use a work email if you might change jobs** — use a personal 
  email
- **Don't use a temporary email service** — you'll need ongoing access 
  for order updates and password recovery
- **Have a strong password ready** — see password rules below

## Step-by-Step: Creating Your Account

### Option 1: Sign Up Directly

1. Go to **voltnest.com/signup**
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a password (rules below)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. Check your email for a verification link
6. Click the verification link
7. You're in — you'll be redirected to your account dashboard

### Option 2: Sign Up During Checkout

If you started shopping without an account:

1. Add items to your cart
2. Proceed to checkout
3. When prompted, enter your email and create a password
4. Complete checkout
5. Verify your email afterward by clicking the link sent to your inbox

**Note**: Your account is created immediately, but you **must verify 
your email** to place future orders and access account features.

## Email Verification

After signup, you'll receive a **verification email** from 
**noreply@voltnest.com** with subject "Verify your VoltNest account."

### Steps
1. Open the email
2. Click the **"Verify Email"** button
3. You'll see a confirmation page
4. You can now shop and manage your account

### Verification link validity
Verification links are valid for **7 days**. If yours expires, log in 
and click **"Resend Verification Email"** on your account dashboard.

### Not receiving the verification email?
- Check your **spam/junk folder**
- Search your inbox for "VoltNest" or "noreply@voltnest.com"
- Wait 15 minutes — some email providers delay system emails
- Check that you entered the correct email (typos are common)
- Whitelist **noreply@voltnest.com** and request a new email

If it still doesn't arrive after 30 minutes, contact 
support@voltnest.com.

## Password Rules

Your password must:

- Be at least **8 characters** long
- Contain at least **1 uppercase letter**
- Contain at least **1 lowercase letter**
- Contain at least **1 number**
- **No spaces**

Your password can:

- Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)
- Be as long as you want (up to 128 characters)

### Password best practices
- Use a **unique password** — never reuse your email password
- Consider a **password manager** (1Password, Bitwarden, Dashlane)
- Don't include personal info (birthday, name, phone)
- Avoid common patterns (12345678, Password1, Qwerty123)

### Examples

| Password | Valid? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `Password1` | ✅ Valid | Meets all requirements |
| `Tr0ub4dor&3` | ✅ Valid | Strong and unique |
| `password` | ❌ Invalid | No uppercase, no number |
| `Pass1` | ❌ Invalid | Too short (5 characters) |
| `P@ss word1` | ❌ Invalid | Contains a space |
| `12345678` | ❌ Invalid | No letters |

## After Creating Your Account

### Recommended next steps

1. **Verify your email** — click the link in the verification email
2. **Add your shipping address** — Account → Addresses → Add New
3. **Add a payment method** (optional) — Account → Payment Methods
4. **Enable 2FA** — strongly recommended — see [Enable 2FA](./enable_2fa.md)
5. **Set your birthday** (optional) — gets you 500 birthday points 
   (see [Loyalty FAQ](../faqs/loyalty_faq.md))
6. **Join the newsletter** (optional) — 200 one-time loyalty points

## Common Signup Problems

### "This email is already registered"

An account already exists with that email. Options:

1. **Log in** at voltnest.com/login — use "Forgot password?" if needed
2. **Reset the password** if you don't remember it (see 
   [Reset Password](./reset_password.md))
3. **Check your inbox** — you may have created an account during a 
   previous order

If you're sure you've never created an account with that email, 
contact support@voltnest.com — there may be a duplicate-email issue.

### "Password doesn't meet requirements"

Check that your password has:
- 8+ characters
- 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 number
- No spaces

See Password Rules above.

### "Verification email isn't arriving"

See "Not receiving the verification email?" above.

### "I made a typo in my email during signup"

You cannot change the email on an existing account to a different one 
that's already in use. Options:

1. **If the typo email doesn't exist**: Contact support@voltnest.com 
   with the correct email and proof of identity — we'll correct it
2. **If the typo email exists (belongs to someone else)**: 
   - Create a new account with your correct email
   - Contact support to delete the old (typo) account
   - Loyalty points and order history cannot be transferred

### "The signup form is stuck or won't submit"

- **Refresh** the page and try again
- **Try a different browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- **Disable browser extensions** (ad blockers, privacy tools)
- **Clear cookies** for voltnest.com
- **Try incognito mode** — if it works there, an extension is 
  interfering
- **Try a different device**

If it still fails, contact support@voltnest.com.

### "I'm not sure if I have an account"

Try:
1. **Go to voltnest.com/login** and enter your email
2. If it says "Invalid credentials," click "Forgot password?"
3. If a password reset email arrives → you have an account
4. If no email arrives after 15 minutes → you probably don't have an 
   account

## Account Types

VoltNest has **one account type** — no business or personal 
distinction. All accounts have the same features:

- Full shopping access
- Order history
- Loyalty program
- Support access

**For business or wholesale orders** (100+ units), see 
[General FAQ](../faqs/general_faq.md) for the partners@voltnest.com 
process.

## Account Limits

There's a limit of **1 account per email address**. Multiple accounts 
on the same email are not permitted.

If you need multiple accounts (e.g., for personal and business use):

- **Use different email addresses** for each
- **Loyalty points do not combine** across accounts
- **Order history is not shared** across accounts

If you want to merge two accounts you already have, email 
support@voltnest.com — we can merge order histories but not loyalty 
points.

## Duplicate or Incorrect Accounts

If you accidentally created multiple accounts:

**You want to keep one, delete the other**:
1. Log in to the account you want to **delete**
2. Email privacy@voltnest.com from that account's email
3. Subject: "Delete Duplicate Account"
4. We process within 30 days

**You want to merge two accounts**:
1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject "Merge Accounts"
2. Include both account emails
3. We merge order histories (loyalty points don't merge)

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- You can't complete signup due to a technical error
- You made a typo in your email and need it corrected
- You believe your email is registered but you never created an account
- You need to merge or delete a duplicate account
- You need to change the email on your account

### What to Include

1. **The email** you're trying to use (or already used)
2. **The issue** (technical error, typo, duplicate, etc.)
3. **Screenshots** of any error messages
4. **Your full name** (if verification is required)

### How to Contact

- **Email**: support@voltnest.com, subject "Account Signup Issue"
- **Live chat**: voltnest.com/chat

### Response Time

- **Standard**: 24 hours
- **Urgent (blocked from ordering)**: 4 business hours

## What NOT to Do

### ❌ Don't use a temporary email address
You'll lose access to order confirmations, shipping updates, and 
password recovery.

### ❌ Don't share your account with others
Each account should have one owner. Sharing violates our 
[Terms of Service](../policies/terms_of_service.md) and can lead to 
account suspension.

### ❌ Don't reuse a password from another site
If that site is breached, your VoltNest account becomes vulnerable.

### ❌ Don't enter a fake name or birthdate
Birthdate earns you birthday loyalty points. Fake info means you'll 
miss them.

### ❌ Don't create multiple accounts to farm loyalty points
Self-referrals and point-farming violate [Terms of Service](../policies/terms_of_service.md) 
and may result in account suspension.

### ❌ Don't skip email verification
Unverified accounts can't place future orders. Verify within 7 days.

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 agents handling account signup issues.

### Triage Questions
- What email are you using?
- Have you created a VoltNest account before?
- What error are you seeing? (screenshot)
- Did you receive a verification email?
- Have you checked spam/junk folders?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Guiding through signup flow
- Resending verification email
- Password rule clarification
- Signup form technical guidance
- Duplicate email checking

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Email typo correction needed
- Account merge requested
- Duplicate account deletion
- Suspected fraud (many accounts from same IP)
- Verification emails not arriving after multiple resends

### Email Change Protocol
Changing an account email requires:

1. Confirm current email access (verification email to old address)
2. Confirm ownership of new email (verification to new address)
3. Both confirmations completed → email change processed within 1 
   business day

**Never** change an account email based on email alone.

### Duplicate Account Investigation
Before merging or deleting:

1. Verify both accounts are owned by same person (order history, 
   payment methods)
2. Confirm customer knows loyalty points won't transfer
3. Document in CRM before proceeding

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not promise a specific time for account merge
- Do not merge loyalty points — policy is fixed
- Do not delete accounts without verifying ownership
- Do not tell them to "just create a new account" if they have 
  order history
- Do not bypass email verification for any reason

### Fraud Indicators
Watch for these patterns:

- Multiple signups from same IP in short time
- Disposable email domains
- Same name / different emails
- Signups immediately followed by large orders

Escalate suspected fraud to Tier 2 within **1 business hour**.
