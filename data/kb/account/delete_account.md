# Delete Account Guide

> **Category**: account  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Step-by-step guide for permanently deleting your VoltNest account — 
what happens to your data, when it happens, what you lose, and how to 
do it safely. Includes alternatives to deletion if you just need a 
break.

## Overview

Deleting your VoltNest account is **permanent**. Once deleted, it 
cannot be reactivated, and certain data is retained for legal and 
warranty purposes (see "What Happens to Your Data" below).

**Before you delete**, consider alternatives:

| If you want to... | Try this instead |
|---|---|
| Stop marketing emails | Unsubscribe from newsletters |
| Stop using VoltNest for now | Simply stop logging in |
| Protect privacy | Delete specific data (see [Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md)) |
| Fix a bug or issue | Contact support first |

**Deletion is only the right choice if you're sure you don't want to 
shop with VoltNest again.**

## Before You Delete — What You'll Lose

Read this carefully. Some of these cannot be recovered.

### You will lose:

- ❌ **Account access** — cannot log in after deletion
- ❌ **Order history in your account** — no longer visible to you
  (retained internally for 7 years per legal requirements)
- ❌ **Loyalty points** — forfeited immediately, no cash value
- ❌ **Gift card balances** — forfeited if not used before deletion
- ❌ **Saved addresses** — deleted
- ❌ **Saved payment methods** — deleted
- ❌ **Active subscriptions** (if any) — cancelled
- ❌ **Wishlist / saved items** — deleted
- ❌ **Review history** — deleted (reviews may remain on product 
  pages but unlinked from you)
- ❌ **2FA settings** — deleted
- ❌ **Chat transcripts visibility** — you lose access; retained 
  internally for 24 months per policy

### You cannot:

- Reactivate a deleted account
- Transfer loyalty points to another account
- Recover order history
- Recover gift card balance

### What you keep:

- Product warranties on items already purchased remain valid (the 
  warranty is tied to the product, not the account). However, filing 
  a claim becomes harder without account access — you'll need to 
  provide proof of purchase from emails.
- Downloaded order confirmations (if you saved them)

[Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md)

## Section A: Before You Delete (Checklist)

Work through this list to ensure deletion is safe and complete.

### A1. Use up loyalty points
Loyalty points are **forfeited immediately** on deletion. If you have 
points:

- Redeem them for a promo code **before** deleting
- Promo codes remain valid for **90 days** after generation (see 
  [Loyalty FAQ](../faqs/loyalty_faq.md))
- Use the promo code within that window

### A2. Spend remaining gift card balances
Gift card balances are **forfeited** on deletion.

- Check **Account → Gift Cards**
- Use any balance before deleting
- Gift cards themselves are not tied to your account — if you have 
  the code, you can redeem on a new account later

### A3. Download your order history
Once deleted, you lose account access to your order history.

- Go to **Account → Orders → Export Order History**
- Download the CSV
- Save it somewhere safe (personal drive, email to self)

### A4. Cancel any active services
VoltNest doesn't offer subscriptions, but check:

- Any pre-orders you have pending
- Any warranty claims in progress
- Any returns in progress

Wait for these to complete **before** deleting, or you'll lose the 
ability to track them.

### A5. Note pending refunds
If you have a refund in progress:

- Wait for it to complete (5–7 business days typical)
- Refunds go to the **original payment method** regardless of account 
  status, so technically safe — but easier to track while logged in

### A6. Change your email (optional)
If you want to keep using the email address on a fresh account later, 
you can either:

- Use a different email for the new account, or
- Delete the current account first, then create a new one with the 
  same email (may take 30 days for email to be released)

**Most customers don't do this** — if you're deleting, you probably 
don't want to re-register.

## Section B: Delete Your Account

Deletion requires contacting support — there is no self-service delete 
button. This is intentional (prevents accidental deletions and 
account takeovers).

### Steps

1. **Log in** to your VoltNest account
2. **Verify your email** is accessible (you'll receive confirmation)
3. **Email** privacy@voltnest.com from your account email
4. **Subject**: "Delete My Account"
5. **Include in the email**:
   - Your full name (as on the account)
   - Your account email
   - Your most recent order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`) — for 
     verification
   - The statement: "I request permanent deletion of my VoltNest 
     account and all associated personal data."
6. **Send** the email

### What happens next

1. **Within 1 business day**: Support acknowledges your request by 
   email
2. **Within 2 business days**: We verify your identity (using the 
   order ID and account email)
3. **Within 30 days**: Your account is deleted and personal data is 
   removed
4. **After deletion**: You receive a final confirmation email at the 
   account email address

**Total time**: Up to **30 days** from request to completion.

### What we need to verify

To prevent malicious deletion, we verify:

- The email is sent from the account email
- The order ID matches an order on the account
- The full name matches the account

If verification fails, we'll ask for additional confirmation (see 
Section D).

## Section C: What Happens to Your Data

When your account is deleted, different data is handled differently.

### Data deleted within 30 days:

- Name, email, phone number
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Saved payment methods (tokens deleted from Stripe)
- Password and 2FA settings
- Wishlists, saved items, preferences
- Marketing preferences
- Birthday, gender, optional profile fields

### Data retained (cannot be deleted):

Per legal, tax, and warranty requirements, we retain:

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Order history (transaction records) | **7 years** | Tax and legal requirements |
| Chat transcripts | **24 months** | Support quality and dispute resolution |
| Fraud and abuse signals | **5 years** | Fraud prevention |
| Warranty claim records | **12 months from claim resolution** | Warranty tracking |
| Cookie consent records | **3 years** | Legal compliance |

**Retained data**:
- Is anonymized where possible
- Is used only for legal/compliance purposes
- Is not used for marketing
- Cannot be accessed by you (you no longer have an account)
- Cannot be used to reactivate your account

**Full details**: [Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md)

## Section D: If Verification Fails

If we can't verify your identity from the initial request:

### What we'll ask for

- **Additional order IDs** — older orders help
- **Last 4 digits of a payment method** used on the account
- **Billing address on file**
- **Verification email** — we may send a code to your account email

### What we will NOT ask for

- Your password
- Your full card number
- Your 2FA code (except as part of the login you'd already do)
- Your SSN or government ID (unless legally required — rare)

If someone claiming to be VoltNest support asks for any of these, 
it's phishing. Forward to security@voltnest.com.

## Section E: What If You Change Your Mind?

### Before deletion completes (within 30 days)
If you change your mind **before** the 30-day window completes:

1. Reply to the deletion request email immediately
2. State that you want to cancel the deletion
3. We'll halt the process if deletion hasn't completed

### After deletion completes
**Deletion is permanent.** You cannot:

- Reactivate the account
- Recover order history
- Recover loyalty points
- Recover gift card balances

You can create a **new account** with the same or different email — 
but it starts fresh with no history.

### If you changed your email and now can't use it
The deletion email comes from us to your account email. If you've 
already changed the email, you'll need to reply from the new email — 
contact privacy@voltnest.com to coordinate.

## Section F: Alternatives to Deletion

If you don't want to shop at VoltNest but want to keep your options 
open, consider these alternatives.

### F1. Unsubscribe from marketing
Stop all marketing emails without deleting your account:

1. Log in
2. Account → Preferences → Email Settings
3. Toggle off:
   - Promotional offers
   - Product launches
   - Loyalty program updates
   - Newsletter

You'll still receive **transactional emails** (order confirmations, 
shipping updates) if you place orders.

### F2. Just stop using your account
If you don't log in, nothing happens. Your account stays dormant:

- No emails unless you place an order
- Loyalty points still expire after 24 months of inactivity
- Account remains in our system (per retention rules)

### F3. Delete specific data only
You don't have to delete the whole account. You can request:

- Deletion of specific addresses
- Removal of saved payment methods
- Deletion of chat history
- Removal from marketing lists

Email **privacy@voltnest.com** with a specific request.

### F4. Temporarily deactivate
**Not supported.** VoltNest does not offer temporary deactivation. 
Alternatives: stop logging in, unsubscribe from marketing (F1).

### F5. Delete chat history only
Chat transcripts are retained for 24 months. To request early deletion 
of **your** chat history:

- Email privacy@voltnest.com
- Subject: "Delete Chat History"
- This does not delete your account

**Note**: Some chat data may be retained for ongoing disputes or 
fraud investigations.

### F6. Update your data instead
If your concern is **data accuracy** (wrong address, old phone number), 
you can update it instead:

- Account → Profile → edit any field
- No need to delete

## Section G: Common Questions

### Will deleting my account affect my warranties?
No. Warranties are tied to the product and purchase date, not your 
account. However, filing a warranty claim without account access 
requires emailing support@voltnest.com with proof of purchase (order 
confirmation email).

### What about orders in transit?
If you have orders in transit:

1. Wait for delivery
2. Then delete

If you delete before delivery, you lose order tracking — but the 
package still ships to the address on the order.

### What about returns in progress?
Wait for returns to complete before deleting. The refund still 
processes, but you won't be able to track it.

### Can I keep my loyalty points on a new account?
No. Loyalty points are tied to the account and are forfeited on 
deletion.

### Can I transfer my order history to a new account?
No. Order history is not transferable.

### Can I delete my account if I have unpaid balances?
Yes. Deletion is your right, regardless of account balance. Contact 
support if you need to settle anything first (e.g., a returned item 
in transit).

### Can someone else delete my account?
Only you can request deletion from your account email. We do not 
process deletion requests from third parties (including family 
members) without a legal order.

### What if I can't access my account email?
You **must** be able to receive email at the account email to verify 
deletion. If you've lost access:

1. First, try to recover your email account (contact your email 
   provider)
2. If unrecoverable, contact privacy@voltnest.com with an explanation 
   and alternative verification

We'll work with you using order history, payment method, and billing 
address to verify ownership.

### How long does deletion take?
Up to **30 days** from the request. Most are completed faster.

### Can I speed up deletion?
No. The 30-day window is required for legal and data-processing 
reasons. Please don't send follow-up emails — they slow processing.

## When to Contact Support

Contact support if:

- You've sent a deletion request but haven't received acknowledgment 
  in 2 business days
- You can't access your account email but want to delete
- You want to cancel a pending deletion (within 30 days)
- You need to delete specific data without deleting the account
- You're not sure if deletion is the right choice — we can discuss 
  options

### What to Include

1. **Account email**
2. **Full name**
3. **Order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
4. **Request type**: Full deletion, partial data, cancel deletion, 
   etc.
5. **Your reason** (optional but helpful for improving our service)

### How to Contact

- **Email**: privacy@voltnest.com
- **Subject lines**:
  - "Delete My Account" — full deletion
  - "Delete Chat History" — chat only
  - "Cancel Deletion Request" — if you changed your mind
  - "Data Request" — for access/copies

### Response Time

- **Acknowledgment**: 1 business day
- **Identity verification**: 2 business days
- **Full deletion**: Up to 30 days
- **Specific data requests**: Up to 30 days (GDPR/CCPA compliant)

## What NOT to Do

### ❌ Don't delete if you have pending orders, returns, or warranty claims
Wait for these to complete. Otherwise you'll lose visibility into 
their status.

### ❌ Don't delete if you haven't used your loyalty points or gift cards
These are forfeited immediately. Redeem or spend them first.

### ❌ Don't delete to "fix" a bug or account issue
Contact support first — most issues can be fixed without deleting.

### ❌ Don't send multiple deletion requests
One is enough. Multiple requests slow processing and may trigger 
manual review.

### ❌ Don't share your password or 2FA code during deletion
We never ask for either. Only account email + order ID are needed.

### ❌ Don't delete because of a phishing email
If you received a suspicious email pretending to be VoltNest, report 
it to security@voltnest.com. Don't delete your real account.

### ❌ Don't expect instant deletion
The 30-day window is required. Plan accordingly.

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 and Tier 2 agents handling deletion requests.

### Triage Questions
- Are you sure you want to delete your account? (confirm understanding 
  of losses)
- Do you have pending orders, returns, or warranty claims?
- Have you used your loyalty points and gift card balances?
- Do you have access to the account email?
- Is this a full deletion or a partial data request?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Explaining the deletion process and timing
- Confirming data retention rules
- Guiding to alternatives (unsubscribe, stop using)
- Forwarding the request to privacy@voltnest.com
- Confirming acknowledgment and verifying order ID

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Customer cannot access account email
- Legal hold or dispute is active on the account
- Multiple requests / customer is confused
- Account has high-value history (large order volume)
- Suspected fraud or coercion (someone forcing deletion)

### Identity Verification Required
Before deletion, verify:

- Email sent from account email
- Full name matches account
- At least one order ID matches
- (If available) last 4 digits of a payment method

**Never** process deletion from a third party (family member, 
attorney) without a legal order.

### Deletion Process (Backend)
Once verified:

1. Tag account for deletion in CRM
2. Notify privacy team
3. Privacy team executes deletion within 30 days
4. Send confirmation email to the account email
5. Do NOT retain any personal data beyond the documented retention 
   rules

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not promise a specific deletion date beyond the 30-day window
- Do not tell them we can recover loyalty points, order history, or 
  gift cards after deletion
- Do not process a partial deletion as a full deletion (or vice versa)
- Do not share the retained data with the customer (they no longer 
  have access)
- Do not delete if there's an active fraud investigation on the account

### Legal Holds
If the account is under a legal hold (subpoena, dispute, tax 
investigation):

- Do not delete
- Escalate to privacy@voltnest.com immediately
- Notify customer that deletion cannot proceed while a hold is active
- Provide legal@voltnest.com as contact for legal inquiries

### Recovering Deleted Accounts
**Not possible.** Once deletion is complete, the account is gone. If 
a customer contacts us after deletion asking to restore their account, 
explain:

- Deletion is permanent per our policy
- They can create a new account
- No data (points, history, addresses) will transfer

### Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR)
For GDPR/CCPA-style "give me my data" requests (separate from 
deletion):

1. Forward to privacy@voltnest.com
2. Response within **30 days** (45 for complex)
3. Include all personal data we hold
4. Do not include internal notes, fraud signals, or other customers' 
   data
