# International FAQ

> **Category**: faqs  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Answers to common questions from international customers — shipping 
destinations, customs and duties, currency, voltage compatibility, 
international returns, and warranty coverage outside the United States.

## Shipping Destinations

### Which countries does VoltNest ship to?

VoltNest ships to **12 international markets**:

- **North America**: Canada
- **Europe**: United Kingdom and all European Union member states
- **Asia-Pacific**: Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore
- **South Asia**: India

### Do you ship to my country?

If your country is listed above, yes. If not, we don't currently ship 
there. We're expanding to new countries periodically — check back or 
email support@voltnest.com to request your country.

### Can I ship to a country different from my billing address?

Yes. You can ship to any supported country regardless of your billing 
address. The shipping address determines delivery; the billing address 
is only used for payment verification.

### Can I ship to multiple international addresses in one order?

No. Each order ships to a single address. Place separate orders for 
multiple destinations.

### Can I ship to a PO box internationally?

No. International orders cannot ship to PO boxes. A physical street 
address is required for customs clearance.

### How long does international shipping take?

Delivery times are listed in the table above. These are estimates — 
actual delivery can be extended by:

- **Customs clearance** (varies by country, can add 3–10 days)
- **Local carrier delays** after customs
- **Weather or carrier disruptions**
- **Peak season** (holidays, Black Friday)

Delivery time does not include our **1–2 business day** processing 
time.

### Do you offer express international shipping?

No. International orders ship via standard international mail only. 
Express options are US-only.

## Customs, Duties, and Taxes

### Will I be charged customs fees or import duties?

Yes, usually. When your order arrives in your country:

- **Customs agencies** may charge import duties based on product 
  category and value
- **Local tax authorities** may charge VAT or GST
- **Carriers** may charge a handling fee for customs processing

These fees are **the customer's responsibility**. VoltNest does not 
prepay or reimburse customs charges.

### How much will I be charged in customs?

Customs charges vary by country. Common examples:

| Country | Typical Duty | VAT / GST |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | 0–8% | 5% GST (+ provincial) |
| UK | 0–12% | 20% VAT |
| EU | 0–14% | 17–27% VAT (varies by country) |
| Australia | 0–10% | 10% GST |
| New Zealand | 0–10% | 15% GST |
| Japan | 0–15% | 10% consumption tax |
| Singapore | 0% (under S$400) | 9% GST |
| India | 0–28% | 18% GST |

**Note**: Orders under certain thresholds may be exempt. Check your 
country's customs authority for exact rules.

### Can VoltNest mark my order as a "gift" to avoid customs?

No. This is **illegal** and would violate customs regulations in your 
country. We declare all orders at their true value on customs forms.

### Why was I charged more than I expected at delivery?

The extra charge is usually customs duty, VAT, or a carrier handling 
fee. These are collected by your country's customs agency, not by 
VoltNest.

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact the carrier or your 
local customs authority — we cannot adjust customs fees.

### What if I refuse to pay customs?

If you refuse to pay customs duties, the package is returned to us. 
In that case:

- Shipping fees are **not refunded**
- A **15% restocking fee** applies for refused international deliveries
- The item cost (minus fees) is refunded once we receive the package

See the [Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md) for 
details.

## Products That Cannot Ship Internationally

### Are any products unavailable for international shipping?

Yes. **VoltBank 20K Power Bank** cannot ship internationally due to 
lithium battery regulations enforced by air carriers and customs 
agencies worldwide.

If your cart contains the VoltBank 20K and you select an international 
address, checkout will block the order. Remove the item to proceed.

### Why can't the power bank ship internationally?

Lithium-ion batteries above certain capacities are restricted on 
international air freight. Our carrier partners do not accept 
international shipments containing these batteries.

This restriction is set by carriers and regulators, not by VoltNest.

### Are there other shipping restrictions?

Yes:

- Orders over **$500 USD** cannot ship internationally
- Orders to **freight forwarders** are not accepted
- Orders to **military installations** outside the US are not accepted

## Currency & Payment

### What currency are prices in?

All prices are in **US Dollars (USD)**. If you're shopping from another 
country, your bank or payment provider will convert at their rate.

### Can I pay in my local currency?

No. VoltNest charges in USD only. Your bank converts to your local 
currency at their rate.

### Will I be charged a foreign transaction fee?

Possibly. Many banks charge a **0–3% foreign transaction fee** for 
purchases in a foreign currency. This is charged by your bank, not by 
VoltNest.

If you want to avoid this, use a card with no foreign transaction 
fees (many travel cards offer this).

### Which payment methods work for international orders?

All accepted methods work internationally:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay / Google Pay
- Klarna / Afterpay

**Note**: Klarna and Afterpay availability varies by country. Check 
their respective websites for your region.

## Voltage & Compatibility

### Will VoltNest electronics work in my country?

Yes — all VoltNest electronics are designed for **universal voltage** 
(100–240V, 50/60 Hz). This means they work worldwide without a voltage 
converter.

### Do I need a plug adapter?

Yes. VoltNest products ship with a **US-style plug** (Type A/B). For 
use in other countries, you'll need a plug adapter for your local 
outlet type:

| Region | Plug Type |
|---|---|
| UK | Type G |
| EU | Type C / F |
| Australia, NZ | Type I |
| India | Type D / M |
| Japan | Type A (same as US) |

Plug adapters are inexpensive and available at any electronics store. 
They do **not** change voltage — just the plug shape.

### Does the VoltCharge 65W support international voltages?

Yes. Input voltage is 100–240V, 50/60 Hz — universal. You can use it 
in any country with a plug adapter.

### Do Bluetooth devices work internationally?

Yes. Bluetooth is a global standard with no regional restrictions. 
VoltBuds Pro, VoltBoom, and VoltFit Band all work internationally.

## International Returns

### Can I return an item from outside the US?

Yes. International customers follow the same **30-day return window** 
as US customers, but:

- You pay return shipping at the actual carrier cost (typically 
  $25–$60 USD depending on country)
- Import duties and taxes paid to your customs agency are **not 
  refundable** by VoltNest
- International returns must be shipped within **14 days** of RMA 
  issuance
- Refunds may take **3–5 additional business days** for international 
  banking

See the [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md) 
for the full process.

### Who pays for return shipping on defective items?

If the item is defective, damaged in transit, or was the wrong item, 
VoltNest covers return shipping. We'll either:

- Provide a prepaid international label (if available to your country), or
- Reimburse reasonable return shipping up to **$30 USD**

### Do I need to pay customs again on a return?

Possibly. When you ship an item back to the US, some countries may 
require you to declare it as a return. Work with your carrier to 
ensure proper documentation.

VoltNest cannot reimburse customs fees on returns.

### Can I return internationally to a local address?

No. All returns must be shipped to our returns center in the United 
States. We do not have local return hubs in other countries.

## International Warranty

### Is my warranty valid if I bought from outside the US?

Yes. All VoltNest products carry a **12-month limited warranty** 
regardless of where the customer is located. See the 
[Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty_policy.md) for details.

### How do I file a warranty claim from outside the US?

Same process as US customers:

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject "Warranty Claim"
2. Include order ID, product SKU, description, and photos/video
3. We'll review and provide a resolution

If the product needs to be shipped back:

- VoltNest reimburses reasonable international return shipping up to 
  **$30 USD**
- Replacements ship from our US warehouse to your address (subject to 
  shipping costs and customs)

### Does the warranty cover customs fees for replacements?

No. If we ship a replacement internationally, customs fees are the 
customer's responsibility. We don't reimburse customs on warranty 
replacements.

## Tracking International Orders

### How do I track my international order?

Tracking works the same as domestic:

1. Check the shipping confirmation email for your tracking number
2. Track on the carrier's website (UPS, DHL, or your local postal 
   service)
3. Or log in to **Account → Orders** to see the current status

### Why hasn't my tracking updated in weeks?

International tracking can have gaps:

- **Before customs**: updates every 1–2 days
- **During customs**: may not update for 5–10 days
- **After customs**: local carrier takes over; tracking may switch to 
  a different provider

If tracking hasn't updated in **21 business days**, contact 
support@voltnest.com to open an investigation.

### My package is stuck in customs — what do I do?

Customs delays are common and often unavoidable. What you can do:

1. Contact your local customs agency with your tracking number
2. If they need documents from you, provide them promptly
3. If customs requests payment, pay promptly to avoid return

VoltNest cannot speed up customs processing. If your package is 
returned due to unpaid customs, see "What if I refuse to pay customs?" 
above.

### Can I get a refund if my international package is lost?

Yes. If a package is confirmed lost in transit:

- **No tracking update for 21 business days** → considered lost
- VoltNest files a claim with the carrier
- You receive a **full refund or free replacement** (your choice)
- Process completes within **10 business days**
