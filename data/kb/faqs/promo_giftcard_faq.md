# Promo & Gift Card FAQ

> **Category**: faqs  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Answers to the most common questions about promo codes, discount codes, 
and VoltNest gift cards — how to use them, why they might not work, 
stacking rules, and what happens to them on returns.

## Promo Codes — Basics

### What's the difference between a promo code, a coupon, and a discount code?

Nothing. We use the terms interchangeably. A promo code, coupon, 
discount code, or voucher is the same thing: a short alphanumeric 
code you enter at checkout for a price reduction.

### How do I apply a promo code?

At checkout, look for the **"Promo Code"** field (usually below the 
order summary). Enter the code and click **Apply**. The discount 
appears immediately in your total.

If you've already placed the order, promo codes **cannot be applied 
retroactively**. Cancel and reorder with the code to get the discount.

### Where do I find promo codes?

VoltNest promo codes come from:

- **Welcome email** — new customers get a one-time code
- **Newsletter signup** — occasional subscriber-only codes
- **Loyalty program** — redeem points for codes (see [Loyalty FAQ](./loyalty_faq.md))
- **Seasonal campaigns** — Black Friday, Cyber Monday, etc.
- **Abandoned cart email** — for customers who leave items in cart
- **Refer-a-friend** — after a friend makes their first purchase

We do **not** post codes on coupon aggregator sites (RetailMeNot, 
Honey, etc.). Codes you find there are often expired or fake.

### Where can I find current promo codes?

Legitimate sources:

- **voltnest.com/promos** — official promotions page
- **Email inbox** — check the email address on your account
- **Your account** — see "My Offers" in Account settings
- **Our social media** — @VoltNestHelp on Twitter/X

If you're unsure whether a code is legitimate, contact 
support@voltnest.com.

## Promo Codes — Rules

### Can I stack multiple promo codes?

No. **One promo code per order.** You cannot combine two promo codes, 
even if they apply to different items.

### Can I stack a promo code with a gift card?

Yes. Gift cards and promo codes **can be combined**. The promo code 
applies first, then the gift card covers the remaining balance.

### Can I stack a promo code with a loyalty reward?

No. Loyalty rewards are issued as promo codes. Since you can only use 
one promo code per order, you can't combine a loyalty reward with 
another promo code.

### Can I use the same promo code multiple times?

Usually not — most codes are **one use per customer**. Some codes 
(especially loyalty rewards) may allow one use only. The specific 
limit is stated in the code's terms.

### Can I use a promo code on a sale item?

Depends on the code. Most codes **cannot** be combined with other 
discounts, including sale prices. The code's terms will state this. 
If a code is described as "sitewide," it typically works on sale items 
too — but not always.

### Can I use a promo code on gift cards?

No. Promo codes cannot be applied to the purchase of gift cards.

### Can I use a promo code on taxes or shipping?

No. Promo codes apply to product subtotals only. Shipping and taxes 
are calculated after the discount.

### Do promo codes work on international orders?

Yes, unless the code's terms specify "US only." International 
customers can use most promo codes.

## Promo Codes — Troubleshooting

### Why isn't my promo code working?

Common reasons:

1. **Expired** — check the expiry date in the email or promo page
2. **Already used** — most codes are one use per customer
3. **Wrong products** — some codes only apply to specific categories
4. **Minimum not met** — some codes require a minimum order total
5. **Not stackable** — the code can't combine with other discounts
6. **Typo** — codes are case-insensitive but spacing matters
7. **Region-restricted** — some codes are US-only
8. **New customer only** — some codes are for first-time buyers

If none of these apply, contact support@voltnest.com with:

- The code
- A screenshot of the error message
- Your cart contents

### The code says "invalid" but I just received it — why?

Possible causes:

- **Email delay** — some codes activate 1 hour after the email goes out
- **Wrong email** — if forwarded, the code may be tied to the original 
  recipient's account
- **Region issue** — the code may be US-only and you're shipping 
  internationally
- **Case sensitivity** — some codes are case-sensitive (rare but possible)

Try again in an hour. If it still fails, contact support.

### Can I apply a promo code after placing my order?

No. Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively. If you found a code 
after ordering, cancel the order (if it hasn't shipped) and reorder 
with the code. See the [Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md).

### The code didn't work — can you apply it manually?

Support can **rarely** apply codes manually, only if:

- The code is valid, unexpired, and unused
- You placed the order within the last 24 hours
- The order hasn't shipped

If eligible, contact support@voltnest.com with your order ID and the 
code. We can issue the discount as a partial refund in some cases.

### Can I get a refund if a code I used goes on sale later?

No. Promo codes are applied at the time of purchase. If a better code 
becomes available later, we cannot retroactively apply it.

## Promo Codes — Expiry & Refunds

### Do promo codes expire?

Yes — all promo codes have an expiry date. The expiry is stated in the 
email or promo page where you received the code. Codes typically last 
between **7 days and 30 days** from issue.

### What happens if I return an item bought with a promo code?

When you return an item purchased with a promo code:

- The **refund is calculated on what you paid**, not the original price
- The promo discount is **not refunded in cash**
- Example: $100 item, $20 promo → you paid $80 → your refund is $80

If the return causes your order total to fall below a minimum threshold, 
the promo may become invalid, and the full discount could be deducted 
from your refund. See the [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md).

### Can I get a promo code reissued if it expires before I use it?

No. Promo codes expire on the stated date and are not reissued. Contact 
support only if you have a specific circumstance — we evaluate 
case-by-case.

## Gift Cards — Basics

### Can I buy a VoltNest gift card?

Yes. Gift cards are available at **voltnest.com/gift-cards** in 
denominations of:

- $25
- $50
- $100
- $200
- Custom amount ($25–$500)

### Are gift cards digital or physical?

Both. **Digital gift cards** are delivered by email within minutes of 
purchase. **Physical gift cards** are shipped by mail and take 5–7 
business days.

Physical gift cards come in a small gift box with a printed message 
card (optional).

### Can I schedule a gift card to arrive on a specific date?

Yes — for digital gift cards, you can schedule the delivery email for 
any future date up to **6 months** in advance.

### Can I add a personal message to a gift card?

Yes. At checkout, you can add a message of up to **250 characters** 
that appears in the delivery email (or on the printed card for physical 
gift cards).

### Do gift cards expire?

No. VoltNest gift cards **never expire**. Balances remain valid 
indefinitely.

### Are there fees for buying or using gift cards?

No. No purchase fees, no activation fees, no monthly maintenance fees, 
no dormancy fees. The amount you pay is the amount on the card.

## Gift Cards — Redemption

### How do I use a gift card?

At checkout, enter the gift card code in the **"Gift Card"** field and 
click **Apply**. The balance is deducted from your order total.

If the gift card balance is more than your order total, the remaining 
balance stays on the card for future purchases.

If the order total is more than the gift card balance, you pay the 
difference with any accepted payment method.

### Can I use more than one gift card on a single order?

Yes. You can apply **multiple gift cards** to a single order — up to 
5 gift cards per order.

### Can I use a gift card and a promo code on the same order?

Yes. Promo code applies first, then the gift card covers the remaining 
balance.

### Can I use a gift card to buy another gift card?

No. Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards.

### Can I use a gift card to pay for shipping and taxes?

Yes. Gift cards cover the full order total, including shipping and 
taxes.

### Can I use a gift card on international orders?

Yes. Gift cards work globally, but all balances are in **USD**. 
International currency conversion is handled by your bank.

## Gift Cards — Balance & Management

### How do I check my gift card balance?

Go to **voltnest.com/gift-card-balance** and enter the code. You'll 
see the remaining balance instantly. You can also see all your 
registered gift cards in **Account → Gift Cards**.

### Can I reload a gift card?

No. Gift cards are one-time load only. If you want to add more value, 
purchase an additional gift card.

### Can I transfer a gift card to someone else?

Yes. Gift cards are not tied to any account — they're effectively cash. 
Share the code with anyone, and they can redeem it.

### What if I lose my gift card code?

If you bought the card and have the confirmation email, you can 
retrieve the code from **Account → Gift Cards**. If you received the 
card as a gift and lost the code, we cannot recover it — treat gift 
card codes like cash.

### Can I get a refund on a gift card?

No. Gift cards are non-refundable once purchased, except in cases of 
error (duplicate charge, wrong amount). Contact support@voltnest.com 
within 30 days if you believe your purchase was in error.

### What happens to my gift card if I return an item?

When you return an item:

- **Refund goes to the original payment method**
- If you paid with a gift card, the refund goes back to a **new gift 
  card** (with a new code)
- The new gift card is emailed within **1 business day** of return approval

You cannot choose to receive a cash refund for a gift-card purchase.

### What if I return an item and my gift card balance is exhausted?

If you paid partially with a gift card and partially with another 
method, the refund is split proportionally:

- Gift card portion → new gift card
- Card/PayPal portion → refunded to that method

### Can I merge two gift cards?

No. You can use up to 5 gift cards on a single order, but you cannot 
combine them into one.

### My gift card says "already redeemed" — what now?

If you believe a gift card was redeemed without your authorization:

1. Contact support@voltnest.com immediately
2. Provide the gift card code and a screenshot of the error
3. We'll investigate within **2 business days**

If the redemption was fraudulent, we may issue a replacement. This is 
rare and evaluated case-by-case.

## Refer-a-Friend

### How does refer-a-friend work?

Share your unique referral link (found in **Account → Refer a Friend**):

- Your friend gets **$10 off** their first order over $50
- You get **$10 in loyalty points** after their first order ships

### Can I refer multiple friends?

Yes. There's no limit. You earn $10 in loyalty points for each friend 
who makes a qualifying first purchase.

### Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals violate our [Terms of Service](../policies/terms_of_service.md) 
and may result in account suspension.

### What counts as a "qualifying" first purchase?

Your friend must:

- Be a new customer (never ordered before)
- Use your referral link
- Place an order of $50 or more
- Complete the order (not cancel or return)

Points are issued after the order ships, not when placed.
