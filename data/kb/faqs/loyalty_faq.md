# Loyalty FAQ (VoltNest Rewards)

> **Category**: faqs  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Answers to common questions about **VoltNest Rewards** — our free 
loyalty program. Earn points on every purchase, referral, and review, 
then redeem them for discounts. This page explains how it works.

## Program Basics

### What is VoltNest Rewards?

VoltNest Rewards is our free loyalty program. You earn **points** for 
every dollar you spend, plus bonus points for referrals, reviews, and 
special events. Points convert to **promo codes** you can use at 
checkout.

### Is there a cost to join?

No. VoltNest Rewards is **completely free**. There are no membership 
fees, no minimum purchases, and no annual renewal.

### How do I join?

You're automatically enrolled when you create a VoltNest account. 
There's nothing to sign up for separately. To see your points balance, 
go to **Account → VoltNest Rewards**.

### Do I need an account to earn points?

Yes. You must be logged in when placing orders to earn points. Guest 
checkout is not available at VoltNest, so all orders earn points 
automatically.

### Does my loyalty program work internationally?

Yes. VoltNest Rewards works for all customers, regardless of location. 
Points are earned in USD-equivalent value.

## Earning Points

### How do I earn points?

There are 5 ways to earn:

| Action | Points Earned |
|---|---|
| Purchase | 10 points per $1 spent |
| Product review (with photo) | 50 points |
| Refer a friend | 1,000 points (after their first order ships) |
| Birthday bonus | 500 points (on your birthday) |
| Newsletter signup | 200 points (one-time) |

### How many points do I earn per dollar spent?

**10 points per $1 spent.** For example:

- $25 order → 250 points
- $50 order → 500 points
- $100 order → 1,000 points

Points are calculated on the **product subtotal**, excluding shipping, 
taxes, and any discounts applied.

### Do I earn points on shipping and taxes?

No. Points are earned on the product subtotal only. Shipping and taxes 
do not earn points.

### Do I earn points on orders paid with a gift card?

Yes. As long as you're logged in, you earn points regardless of the 
payment method — including gift cards and loyalty-redeemed discounts.

### Do I earn points on discounted items?

Yes, but only on the **amount you actually paid**. If a $100 item is 
discounted to $80, you earn 800 points (10 points × $80).

### Do I earn points on returns?

No. If you return an item, the points earned on that item are 
**deducted from your balance**. If your balance goes negative, it will 
be restored to 0, and future points will first cover the deficit.

### How do I earn points for a review?

After purchasing a product, go to its page and click **"Write a 
Review."** Once published (typically within 48 hours), points are 
credited automatically. Reviews with a **photo** earn the full 50 
points; text-only reviews earn 25 points.

You can only review products you've purchased. One review per product 
per customer.

### How do I earn referral points?

Go to **Account → Refer a Friend** and share your unique link. When 
your friend:

1. Uses your link to create an account
2. Places their **first order of $50 or more**
3. Completes the order (not cancelled or returned)

You earn **1,000 points** ($10 value). Points are credited **after the 
order ships**, not when placed. See the 
[Promo & Gift Card FAQ](./promo_giftcard_faq.md) for more on referrals.

### How do birthday points work?

On your birthday month, you receive **500 points** credited to your 
account — automatic, no action needed. Add your birthday in 
**Account → Profile** to activate this benefit.

Birthday points are credited on the **1st of your birthday month**.

### Do I earn points for signing up for the newsletter?

Yes — **200 points** for your first newsletter subscription. One-time 
only.

### Do I earn points for leaving a support review?

No. Support reviews earn our gratitude but not points.

## Point Value & Redemption

### How much are my points worth?

**100 points = $1 USD.** So:

| Points | Value |
|---|---|
| 500 | $5 |
| 1,000 | $10 |
| 2,500 | $25 |
| 5,000 | $50 |

### How do I redeem points?

1. Go to **Account → VoltNest Rewards → Redeem Points**
2. Choose a redemption amount (minimum 500 points = $5)
3. Click **"Generate Promo Code"**
4. Copy the code
5. Apply it at checkout like any promo code

The code is valid for **90 days** from generation.

### What's the minimum number of points to redeem?

**500 points ($5)** is the minimum. You cannot redeem less.

### Is there a maximum redemption?

Yes. You can redeem up to **50,000 points ($500)** per year. This 
resets on January 1 each year.

### Can I redeem points for cash?

No. Points are redeemable only as promo codes for VoltNest purchases. 
They have no cash value and cannot be transferred, sold, or exchanged.

### Can I use a loyalty promo code with another promo code?

No. Since loyalty rewards are issued as promo codes, and only **one 
promo code** is allowed per order, you cannot combine a loyalty reward 
with another promo code.

You **can** combine a loyalty promo code with:
- Gift cards
- Sale prices (in most cases — check the specific reward's terms)

### Do points expire?

Yes — points expire **24 months (2 years)** after they are earned, if 
unused. Expiration is tracked per earning event, not by account age.

You'll receive an email reminder **30 days before** any points are 
about to expire.

### Can I get expired points back?

No. Expired points cannot be reinstated. Set a reminder or redeem 
your points regularly.

### Can I transfer points to another account?

No. Points are tied to your account and cannot be transferred, gifted, 
or pooled.

## Membership Tiers

### Are there membership tiers?

Yes. VoltNest Rewards has **3 tiers** based on your annual spend 
(rolling 12 months):

| Tier | Annual Spend | Earn Rate | Extra Benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Silver** | $0–$249 | 10 pts/$1 | Standard benefits |
| **Gold** | $250–$999 | 15 pts/$1 | Free express shipping on orders over $50 |
| **Platinum** | $1,000+ | 20 pts/$1 | Free express shipping on all orders, early access to new products |

### How do I know my tier?

Go to **Account → VoltNest Rewards**. Your current tier and progress 
toward the next tier are shown at the top.

### When does my tier update?

Tiers are recalculated **monthly**, based on your rolling 12-month 
spend. If your spend increases, you may upgrade mid-month. If your 
spend decreases, your tier stays for the remainder of the 12-month 
period, then adjusts.

### What are the tier benefits in detail?

**Silver**
- 10 points per $1
- All standard rewards (reviews, referrals, birthday)

**Gold**
- 15 points per $1 (1.5× Silver rate)
- Free express shipping on orders over $50 (saves $12.99 per order)
- Early access to some promotions

**Platinum**
- 20 points per $1 (2× Silver rate)
- Free express shipping on all orders
- Early access to all new product launches
- Priority support (front of queue for chat and email)
- Exclusive quarterly offers

### Can I lose my tier?

Yes, if your rolling 12-month spend drops below the threshold. 
For example:

- You spend $1,200 in year 1 → **Platinum**
- You only spend $400 in year 2 → You drop to **Gold**

Tiers are evaluated monthly, so a drop takes effect on the 1st of the 
following month.

### Do tier benefits stack with other discounts?

Yes. Tier benefits (like free shipping) apply independently of promo 
codes. However, free shipping is based on the shipping method, not a 
discount.

## Double Points Events

### What are double points events?

Several times per year, VoltNest runs **Double Points** or **Triple 
Points** events where you earn 2× or 3× the normal rate. These events 
are announced by email and on our social media.

### How do I know when a double points event is happening?

- **Email** — subscribers receive advance notice
- **Account → VoltNest Rewards** — banner shown during active events
- **Social media** — @VoltNestHelp on Twitter/X

### Do double points apply to all purchases?

Yes, unless the event terms specify otherwise (e.g., "excludes VoltBank 
20K"). The event details are always listed in the announcement.

## Account & Security

### Where can I see my points balance?

Go to **Account → VoltNest Rewards**. Your balance, tier, and recent 
point activity are shown.

### Can I see my points history?

Yes. In the VoltNest Rewards page, click **"View History"** to see all 
points earned and redeemed, with dates and descriptions.

### What happens to my points if I delete my account?

Points are **forfeited immediately** upon account deletion. They cannot 
be restored if you create a new account later.

### What if I think my points balance is wrong?

Contact support@voltnest.com with:

- Your account email
- The order ID or event you believe is missing
- Approximate dates

We'll investigate within **2 business days**.

### Someone used my points — what do I do?

If you notice unauthorized point redemptions:

1. Change your password immediately
2. Log out of all devices
3. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Points Theft"**
4. Include recent point history screenshots

We'll investigate and restore fraudulent redemptions within 
**5 business days**.
