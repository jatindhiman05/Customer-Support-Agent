## Order Changes

### Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, in a limited window. You can modify:

- **Shipping address** — if the order hasn't shipped
- **Items** — add or remove items if the order hasn't been packed
- **Shipping method** — upgrade or downgrade before packing

Once an order has **shipped**, no changes are possible. See the 
[Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md).

### How do I change my shipping address?

Log in and go to **Account → Orders → Select order → "Edit Shipping Address"**. 
This option is available only before the order has shipped.

If the option is unavailable, email support@voltnest.com with your 
order ID and new address. We'll attempt a carrier intercept if the 
order is already in transit (a **$15 fee** applies, and success is not 
guaranteed).

### Can I add items to an existing order?

Yes, if the order hasn't been packed. Go to **Account → Orders → 
"Modify Order"** and add the items. The additional items will ship 
together with your original order.

If the order has already shipped, place a separate order for the 
additional items.

### Can I remove items from an existing order?

Yes, if the order hasn't shipped. Go to **Account → Orders → "Modify 
Order"** and remove items. Shipping fees are recalculated based on the 
new total.

For partial cancellations of multi-item orders, see the 
[Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md).

### Can I change my shipping method after ordering?

Yes, if the order hasn't shipped. Contact support@voltnest.com with:

- Your order ID
- The new shipping method

If the new method costs more, we'll charge the difference. If it costs 
less, we'll refund the difference. **No fees for the change itself.**

### Can I change the email address on my order?

No. The email address on an order cannot be changed once it's placed. 
You can update the email on your account for future orders.

## Order Cancellation

### How do I cancel my order?

You can cancel an order **before it ships**:

1. Log in and go to **Account → Orders → "Cancel Order"**
2. Confirm the cancellation

Self-service cancellation is available until the order enters "Packed" 
status. After that, contact support. See the 
[Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md) for details.

### How long do I have to cancel?

Until **2:00 PM EST** on the day the order was placed — as long as it 
hasn't been packed. Orders placed after 2 PM EST can still be cancelled 
the same day if packing hasn't started, but the window is shorter.

### Can I cancel after my order has shipped?

No. Once shipped, you must wait for delivery and then return the 
items. See the [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md).

### How long does a cancellation refund take?

- **Before packing**: 1 business day
- **After packing**: 2 business days
- **After carrier pickup (intercept)**: 5–7 business days

Full details in the [Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md).

### Can I cancel only part of my order?

Yes, if the order hasn't shipped. See "Can I remove items from an 
existing order?" above.

## Promo Codes (Quick Reference)

### How do I apply a promo code?

Enter the code in the **"Promo Code"** field at checkout and click 
Apply. The discount appears in your order total.

### Can I stack multiple promo codes?

No — **one promo code per order**. See the 
[Promo & Gift Card FAQ](./promo_giftcard_faq.md) for full rules.

### Why isn't my promo code working?

Common reasons:

- The code has expired
- The code is for a specific product category
- You already used the code (one use per customer)
- Minimum order total not met
- The code can't be combined with other discounts

See the [Promo & Gift Card FAQ](./promo_giftcard_faq.md) for details.

## Reviews & Feedback

### How do I leave a product review?

Go to the product page and click **"Write a Review"**. You must have 
purchased the product to review it. Reviews are moderated and typically 
appear within **48 hours**.

### Can I edit or delete my review?

Yes. Log in and go to **Account → My Reviews**. You can edit or delete 
your review at any time.

### Do you offer incentives for reviews?

No. VoltNest does not pay for reviews or offer discounts in exchange 
for positive reviews. We believe honest reviews serve customers better.

### How do I give feedback about my support experience?

After any chat, you'll get a 1-question survey (👍 / 👎). You can also 
email **feedback@voltnest.com** — read by our support manager daily.

### How do I report a product issue?

Email support@voltnest.com with:

- Your order ID
- Product name or SKU
- Description of the issue
- Photos or video

We respond within 24 hours.

## Accessibility

### Is voltnest.com accessible to screen readers?

Yes. We follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. If you encounter an 
accessibility issue, email **accessibility@voltnest.com** and we'll 
address it within 5 business days.

### Do you offer large-print or high-contrast modes?

Yes. Go to **Account → Preferences → Accessibility** to enable:

- High-contrast mode
- Larger text
- Reduced motion
- Screen reader optimizations

### Do you have a phone line for customers with hearing impairments?

Yes. Our phone support (+1 512-555-0142) supports TTY/TDD devices. 
Alternatively, email support@voltnest.com — many customers find email 
easier.

## Business & Wholesale

### Do you offer wholesale or B2B pricing?

Yes. For bulk orders (100+ units), email **partners@voltnest.com** with:

- Your business name and website
- Product interest and estimated volume
- Delivery timeline

We respond within **2 business days**.

### Do you offer corporate gifting?

Yes. For corporate gifting (10+ units with custom packaging), email 
**partners@voltnest.com**. We offer:

- Custom-branded packaging
- Bulk pricing
- Direct shipping to recipients
- Gift message cards

### Can I resell VoltNest products?

Only with written authorization. Email partners@voltnest.com to discuss 
a reseller agreement. Unauthorized resale violates our 
[Terms of Service](../policies/terms_of_service.md).
