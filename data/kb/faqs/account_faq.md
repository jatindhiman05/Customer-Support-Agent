# Account FAQ

> **Category**: faqs  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Answers to the most common questions about VoltNest accounts — signing 
up, logging in, resetting passwords, enabling 2FA, managing your 
profile, and deleting your account. For step-by-step guides, see the 
[Account section](../account/).

## Account Basics

### How do I create a VoltNest account?

Go to **voltnest.com/signup** and enter your email and a password. You'll 
receive a confirmation email within a few minutes — click the link to 
activate your account. You can also create an account automatically 
during your first checkout.

### Do I need an account to place an order?

Yes. VoltNest requires an account for all orders. This lets you track 
orders, request returns, and manage warranty claims. Guest checkout 
is not available.

### Is creating an account free?

Yes — there are no fees, subscriptions, or minimum purchases required 
to have an account.

### What information do I need to sign up?

Just your email address and a password. You can optionally add your 
name, phone number, and shipping address later. We recommend adding 
your shipping address to speed up future checkouts.

### Can I have more than one account?

We recommend one account per person. If you have two accounts and want 
to merge them, email support@voltnest.com — we can merge order 
histories, but not loyalty points.

## Password & Login

### How do I reset my password?

1. Go to **voltnest.com/login**
2. Click **"Forgot password?"**
3. Enter the email on your account
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (arrives within 5 minutes)
5. Click the link and set a new password

If you don't see the email, check your spam folder. If it still hasn't 
arrived after 15 minutes, contact support@voltnest.com.

### I forgot my password — what do I do?

Same process as above: use **"Forgot password?"** on the login page. 
You don't need to remember your old password to set a new one.

### Why am I not receiving the password reset email?

Common reasons:

- **Spam folder** — check there first
- **Wrong email** — did you sign up with a different address?
- **Typo in email** — check for missing dots or letters
- **Email provider delay** — some providers (Outlook, Yahoo) delay 
  system emails by 5–10 minutes

If none of these apply, contact support@voltnest.com.

### My password reset link says "expired" — what now?

Reset links are valid for **1 hour**. If yours expired, go back to 
**voltnest.com/login → "Forgot password?"** and request a new one.

### Can I change my email address?

Yes. Log in and go to **Account → Settings → Email**. You'll need to 
verify both the old and new email addresses before the change takes 
effect.

### Can I change my password without logging in?

No. For security, you must log in to change your password. If you can't 
log in, use the password reset flow instead.

### How do I log out of all devices?

Go to **Account → Security → "Log out of all devices."** This ends 
every active session on all browsers and devices. You'll need to log 
in again on each.

### Why was I logged out automatically?

For security, sessions expire after **30 days of inactivity**. If you 
haven't used your account in 30 days, you'll be asked to log in again. 
Active sessions on trusted devices remain valid.

### Can I stay logged in?

Yes. When you log in, check **"Keep me signed in"** to stay logged in 
for up to 30 days on that device. Don't use this on shared computers.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

### What is 2FA and should I enable it?

Two-factor authentication adds a second verification step (usually a 
6-digit code from your phone) when logging in. It significantly 
improves account security.

We **strongly recommend** enabling 2FA, especially if you store payment 
methods on file or have order history you want to protect.

### How do I enable 2FA?

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Account → Security → Two-Factor Authentication**
3. Choose your method: SMS text or authenticator app
4. Follow the on-screen steps to verify

For step-by-step instructions, see [Enable 2FA](../account/enable_2fa.md).

### What methods of 2FA do you support?

- **Authenticator app** (recommended) — Google Authenticator, Authy, 
  1Password, Microsoft Authenticator
- **SMS text message** — to your registered phone number

### What if I lose my phone with the 2FA app?

Use your **backup codes** (provided when you enabled 2FA). Each backup 
code works once. If you've lost your backup codes:

1. Email support@voltnest.com from the email on your account
2. Provide verification information (last order ID, billing address)
3. We'll disable 2FA so you can log in and re-enable it

Verification typically takes 1 business day.

### Can I disable 2FA?

Yes. Go to **Account → Security → Two-Factor Authentication → Disable**. 
You'll need to enter your password and a current 2FA code to disable it.

## Profile & Preferences

### How do I update my shipping address?

Log in and go to **Account → Addresses → Edit**. You can save multiple 
addresses (home, work, etc.) and choose one at checkout. Address changes 
do not affect orders that have already shipped.

### How do I update my phone number?

Go to **Account → Settings → Phone** and enter the new number. You'll 
receive a verification code by SMS.

### Can I save multiple payment methods?

Yes. Go to **Account → Payment Methods** to add, remove, or set a 
default card. We never store full card numbers — payment details are 
held by Stripe, our PCI-compliant payment processor.

### How do I unsubscribe from marketing emails?

Click the **"Unsubscribe"** link at the bottom of any marketing email. 
Or go to **Account → Preferences → Email Settings** and toggle off 
promotional emails.

You'll still receive transactional emails (order confirmations, 
shipping updates, warranty notices) — these cannot be disabled.

### How do I change my email preferences?

Go to **Account → Preferences → Email Settings**. You can toggle:

- Promotional offers
- Product launches
- Loyalty program updates
- Newsletter

## Order History & Data

### Where can I see my past orders?

Log in and go to **Account → Orders**. You'll see all orders with 
dates, totals, tracking numbers, and status. Orders are retained for 
**7 years** for tax and warranty purposes.

### Can I download my order history?

Yes. Go to **Account → Orders → "Export Order History"** to download 
a CSV file with all your orders. Useful for expense reporting or 
personal records.

### How do I request a copy of my personal data?

Email **privacy@voltnest.com** with your request. We'll provide a 
complete data export within **30 days** (per GDPR/CCPA). See the 
[Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md) for details.

## Deleting Your Account

### How do I delete my account?

Email **privacy@voltnest.com** with subject **"Delete My Account"** 
and include:

- Your account email
- Your most recent order ID (for verification)

We process deletion requests within **30 days**.

### What happens to my data when I delete my account?

- **Profile data** (name, address, preferences) — deleted within 30 days
- **Order history** — retained for 7 years (tax & warranty requirements)
- **Chat transcripts** — retained for 24 months (support quality)
- **Loyalty points** — forfeited immediately on deletion
- **Gift card balances** — forfeited if not used before deletion

See the [Privacy Policy](../policies/privacy_policy.md) for full details.

### Can I reactivate a deleted account?

No. Account deletion is permanent. If you want to shop with us again, 
you'll need to create a new account. Loyalty points and order history 
won't carry over.

### Can I temporarily deactivate instead of deleting?

We don't currently offer temporary deactivation. You can simply stop 
using your account and unsubscribe from emails — your data remains 
until you request deletion.

## Account Security

### Someone accessed my account — what should I do?

1. **Change your password immediately** at voltnest.com/login
2. **Log out of all devices** at Account → Security
3. **Enable 2FA** if not already on
4. **Email support@voltnest.com** with subject "Account Compromise"
5. **Check recent orders** for unauthorized purchases

We'll investigate and take protective action within **4 business hours** 
for verified account compromises.

### How do I know if an email is really from VoltNest?

Legitimate VoltNest emails come from:

- **support@voltnest.com** — support
- **orders@voltnest.com** — order confirmations
- **no-reply@voltnest.com** — automated notices
- **news@voltnest.com** — marketing (with unsubscribe link)

We never ask for your password by email, and we never ask you to click 
a link to "verify your account" outside of the password-reset flow.

If you're unsure about an email, forward it to security@voltnest.com 
before clicking anything.

### How do I report a phishing email pretending to be VoltNest?

Forward the email to **security@voltnest.com**. Don't click any links 
or attachments. We investigate every report and will confirm whether 
it's legitimate within 1 business day.
