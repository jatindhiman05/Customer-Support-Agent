# Payment FAQ

> **Category**: faqs  
> **Audience**: customer  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Answers to the most common questions about paying for VoltNest orders — 
accepted payment methods, declined cards, double charges, installments, 
refunds, and payment security.

## Accepted Payment Methods

### What payment methods do you accept?

VoltNest accepts the following:

- **Credit cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Debit cards**: Visa and Mastercard debit cards
- **PayPal**: Full PayPal checkout
- **Apple Pay**: On iOS and macOS devices
- **Google Pay**: On Android devices and Chrome browser
- **Klarna**: Buy now, pay later (4 interest-free installments)
- **Afterpay**: Buy now, pay later (4 interest-free installments)

### Do you accept prepaid cards?

Yes — Visa, Mastercard, and Amex prepaid cards work if they have a 
registered billing address. Some prepaid cards without a billing 
address may be declined. If your prepaid card fails, try registering 
it with the issuer first.

### Do you accept gift cards?

Yes. VoltNest gift cards can be applied at checkout. See the 
[Promo & Gift Card FAQ](./promo_giftcard_faq.md) for details.

### Do you accept bank transfers, checks, or cash?

No. VoltNest is online-only and accepts only the digital payment 
methods listed above. We do not accept wire transfers, checks, 
money orders, or cash.

### Can I pay in a currency other than USD?

All orders are charged in **US Dollars (USD)**. Your bank or payment 
provider will convert to your local currency at their rate, which may 
include a small conversion fee. See the 
[International FAQ](./international_faq.md) for more.

## Checkout & Payment Issues

### Why was my card declined?

The most common reasons:

- **Insufficient funds** — check your balance
- **Billing address mismatch** — the address you entered doesn't match 
  what your bank has on file
- **CVV incorrect** — the 3-digit code on the back of the card (or 4 
  digits on the front for Amex)
- **Card expired** — check the expiration date
- **Card reported lost or stolen** — the bank has blocked it
- **International transaction block** — your bank may flag online 
  merchants in another country
- **Daily spending limit** — you may have hit your card's limit

Try a different card, or contact your bank to see what triggered the 
decline. VoltNest cannot see why your bank declined a charge — only 
your bank knows.

### Why does my payment keep failing?

If multiple cards fail, the issue is usually:

- Your billing address differs from what your bank has
- Your bank has flagged the transaction
- You're using a VPN or unusual IP address
- 3D Secure verification is failing (see below)

### What is 3D Secure, and why am I being asked to verify?

3D Secure (also called "Verified by Visa" or "Mastercard SecureCode") 
is a bank-mandated security step. When you check out, your bank may 
send you a code by SMS or ask you to verify in your banking app. This 
protects you from fraud.

If 3D Secure fails:

1. Ensure your phone number with the bank is current
2. Check your banking app for a verification prompt
3. Try again in 5 minutes
4. Try a different card

VoltNest cannot bypass 3D Secure — it's controlled by your bank.

## Double Charges & Pending Charges

### Why was I charged twice?

This is almost always a **pending charge**, not a real double charge. 
Here's what happens:

1. You place an order → your bank shows a **pending** charge
2. The pending charge looks like a real charge on some banking apps
3. The order processes and the pending charge is replaced by a real 
   charge

If you see **two charges** and only placed **one order**, the second 
is a pending hold. It will **automatically release within 3–5 business 
days**. You won't be charged twice.

### How do I know if it's a real double charge or a pending hold?

- **Pending**: Shows as "Pending" or "Authorization" in your banking app
- **Real**: Shows as "Posted" or "Completed"

If both charges are posted **and** you placed **one order**, contact 
support@voltnest.com with:

- Your order ID (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- Screenshots of both charges
- The last 4 digits of your card

We'll investigate within **1 business day** and refund any duplicate 
charge immediately.

### Why is there a $1 charge on my card?

Some banks authorize a **$1 test charge** when you save a payment 
method. This is a verification step, not a real charge. It reverses 
within **3–5 business days**.

VoltNest does not charge $1 test fees — this is your bank's process.

## Refunds & Credits

### How long does a refund take?

Refund timing depends on your payment method:

| Payment Method | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit / Debit card | 5–7 business days after approval |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days after approval |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–7 business days after approval |
| Klarna / Afterpay | Adjusted against remaining installments |
| Gift card | Immediate (new gift card issued) |

VoltNest issues refunds immediately upon approval. Remaining time 
depends on your bank or payment provider.

### Where do refunds go?

Refunds always go to the **original payment method**. If you paid 
with a card that's now expired or closed, contact your bank — the 
refund is still delivered to the account, even if the card is 
cancelled.

### Can I get a refund to a different card or bank account?

No. Refunds go to the original payment method only. This is a 
requirement of our payment processor and prevents fraud.

### Why hasn't my refund arrived yet?

If it's been more than **10 business days** since your refund was 
approved:

1. Check your bank statement carefully — refunds sometimes post 
   separately from other transactions
2. Contact your bank — they may be holding the refund for verification
3. If your bank confirms no refund, email support@voltnest.com with 
   your order ID and refund confirmation

We'll investigate within **2 business days**.

## Klarna & Afterpay (Installments)

### How do Klarna and Afterpay work?

Both let you split your purchase into **4 interest-free payments**:

- **Payment 1**: At checkout (25%)
- **Payment 2**: 2 weeks later
- **Payment 3**: 4 weeks later
- **Payment 4**: 6 weeks later

No interest, no fees if you pay on time. Late payments may incur fees 
from Klarna or Afterpay directly.

### What's the minimum order for installments?

Klarna and Afterpay require a minimum order of **$50 USD**.

### What's the maximum order for installments?

Klarna: up to **$1,000 USD**  
Afterpay: up to **$1,500 USD**

Limits may vary based on your account history with each provider.

### Are there fees for using Klarna or Afterpay?

No fees if you pay on time. Late fees are charged by Klarna or 
Afterpay directly:

- **Klarna late fee**: Up to $7 per missed payment
- **Afterpay late fee**: Up to $8 per missed payment, capped at 25% 
  of the order

See Klarna's or Afterpay's terms for full details.

### How do returns work with Klarna or Afterpay?

When you return an item:

1. Klarna or Afterpay pauses remaining installments
2. Once your return is processed, installments are adjusted or cancelled
3. Any payments already made are refunded to your Klarna/Afterpay account

Refunds to Klarna or Afterpay are typically reflected within 
**3–5 business days** of VoltNest approving the return.

### Can I pay off my Klarna or Afterpay balance early?

Yes. Log in to your Klarna or Afterpay account to pay the full 
remaining balance early. There's no prepayment penalty.

## Payment Security

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes. VoltNest uses **Stripe** for all card processing. Stripe is 
**PCI-DSS Level 1** certified — the highest security standard in the 
payment industry.

We **never see or store** your full card number. Stripe handles 
everything.

### Does VoltNest store my card details?

VoltNest stores a **token** from Stripe that lets us charge your card 
for future orders, but we never see or store the actual card number. 
You can remove saved cards at any time in **Account → Payment Methods**.

### Do you use 3D Secure?

Yes. If your bank supports 3D Secure, we'll trigger it during checkout. 
This protects you from unauthorized use of your card.

### How do I remove a saved card?

Log in and go to **Account → Payment Methods → Remove**. The card 
token is deleted from our system, and Stripe's record is purged. 
You can re-add it later if you want.

### Is it safe to save my card?

Yes. Saving your card uses Stripe's tokenization system, which is 
the same technology used by Amazon, Shopify, and most major retailers. 
The card is stored securely and encrypted.

## Sales Tax & Fees

### Do you charge sales tax?

Yes, where legally required. Sales tax is calculated at checkout 
based on your shipping address and applicable state/local laws. 
The tax amount is shown before you confirm payment.

### Do you charge any hidden fees?

No. VoltNest has no hidden fees — no restocking fees, no order 
processing fees, no account fees. The price at checkout is what 
you pay.

### Do you charge currency conversion fees?

No — VoltNest charges in USD only. If your card is in another 
currency, your bank applies its own conversion fee (typically 0–3%).

### Are there fees for international orders?

VoltNest doesn't add international fees, but:

- **Import duties and taxes** are charged by your country's customs 
  agency (not VoltNest)
- **Your bank** may charge a foreign transaction fee (typically 0–3%)

See the [International FAQ](./international_faq.md) for details.
