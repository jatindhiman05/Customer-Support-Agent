# Bluetooth Pairing Issues (Troubleshooting)

> **Category**: troubleshooting  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Diagnostic guide for pairing problems with **VoltBuds Pro** wireless 
earbuds and **VoltBoom** Bluetooth speaker. Covers devices not 
appearing, failed pairing, multipoint conflicts, audio dropout, and 
multi-speaker pairing. Follow the steps in order.

## Symptoms This Guide Covers

Use this guide if you're experiencing any of the following:

- Your device can't find "VoltBuds Pro" or "VoltBoom" in the Bluetooth list
- Pairing starts but fails with an error
- The earbuds or speaker connect then immediately disconnect
- Audio plays through the phone speaker instead of the VoltNest device
- Only one VoltBuds earbud produces sound
- VoltBoom won't connect to a second speaker (Stereo or Party mode)
- Multipoint pairing drops one device when the other connects
- Bluetooth connection is unstable or cuts out frequently
- Previously paired device no longer connects

**Which product do you have?**

- **VoltBuds Pro** → Follow all steps, noting earbud-specific notes
- **VoltBoom** → Follow all steps, noting speaker-specific notes

## Before You Start

Quick facts:

- Both products use **Bluetooth 5.3**
- VoltBuds Pro range: up to **10 meters** (33 feet)
- VoltBoom range: up to **30 meters** (98 feet)
- VoltBuds Pro supports **multipoint** (2 devices)
- VoltBoom supports **multi-speaker** (up to 100 speakers)
- Both require the charging case (earbuds) or power (speaker) to be 
  charged — check this first

**First check — is it charged?**

- **VoltBuds Pro**: Place earbuds in the case and check the LED. If 
  the case battery is below 20%, charging can interrupt pairing. Charge 
  for 30 minutes and try again.
- **VoltBoom**: Press the power button. If no LED lights up, charge 
  for 30 minutes before pairing.

A surprising number of "pairing failures" are actually **dead batteries**.

## Step 1: Basic Pairing (First-Time Setup)

If you've never paired before, follow these steps exactly.

### VoltBuds Pro

1. Open the charging case — the earbuds power on automatically
2. Both earbuds will flash **white** — this means pairing mode
3. On your phone: Settings → Bluetooth → Turn on Bluetooth
4. Wait 5–10 seconds — "VoltBuds Pro" appears in the list
5. Tap it to pair
6. You'll hear a confirmation chime

### VoltBoom

1. Press and hold the power button for **3 seconds** — the LED flashes **blue**
2. On your phone: Settings → Bluetooth → Turn on Bluetooth
3. Wait 5–10 seconds — "VoltBoom" appears in the list
4. Tap it to pair
5. You'll hear a confirmation chime

### If the device doesn't appear
- Wait a full **30 seconds** — some phones scan slowly
- Move the phone and product within **1 meter** of each other
- Turn your phone's Bluetooth **off and back on**
- Continue to Step 2

## Step 2: Forget and Re-Pair

The most common fix for "device not found" or "connection failed" is 
to remove the old pairing and start fresh.

### On your phone
1. Go to Settings → Bluetooth
2. Find "VoltBuds Pro" or "VoltBoom" in the list
3. Tap the "i" or gear icon → **"Forget This Device"**
4. If it's not in the list, skip to Step 3

### On the product
- **VoltBuds Pro**: Place earbuds in the case, close the lid, open it 
  again. Earbuds enter pairing mode automatically.
- **VoltBoom**: Press and hold power for **3 seconds** — you'll hear 
  a prompt tone. Then press and hold **Volume + and Power together 
  for 5 seconds** to enter pairing mode manually. LED flashes blue.

### Then re-pair
Follow Step 1 again. **Do not skip the forget step** — this alone 
resolves most pairing issues.

## Step 3: Factory Reset

If forget-and-repair didn't fix it, do a full factory reset. This 
erases all saved pairings from the product.

### VoltBuds Pro — Factory Reset
1. Place both earbuds in the charging case
2. Keep the case **open**
3. Press and hold the button on the **back of the case** for **15 seconds**
4. LED flashes **red, then white** — release the button
5. Close and reopen the case — earbuds are now in fresh pairing mode

### VoltBoom — Factory Reset
1. Turn on the speaker
2. Press and hold **Power + Bluetooth button** together for **10 seconds**
3. LED flashes **red, then blue** — release
4. Speaker restarts in fresh pairing mode

After a factory reset, re-pair from your phone as if it's a new device 
(Step 1). You'll need to re-pair **every** device that was connected 
before — the reset clears all saved pairings.

## Step 4: Pairing with Two Devices (Multipoint — VoltBuds Pro Only)

VoltBuds Pro supports **multipoint pairing** — connected to 2 devices 
simultaneously (e.g., phone + laptop). VoltBoom does **not** support 
multipoint.

### Enabling multipoint
1. Pair with the **first device** (Step 1)
2. Turn off Bluetooth on the first device (or move out of range)
3. Pair with the **second device** (Step 1)
4. Turn Bluetooth back on the first device

Both devices are now saved. To switch between them:

- **Automatic**: When a call comes in on either device, earbuds switch 
  to it automatically
- **Manual**: Pause playback on one device, start on the other

### Troubleshooting multipoint
- **Only one device plays at a time** — that's correct, only one can 
  play audio at once
- **Second device won't connect** — remove pairing from the second 
  device, then re-pair
- **Frequent switching glitches** — forget both devices and re-pair 
  in order (first device, then second)
- **Cannot pair a third device** — multipoint supports 2 maximum; 
  forget one to add another

## Step 5: Pairing Multiple VoltBoom Speakers

VoltBoom has two multi-speaker modes. **Do not confuse them.**

### Stereo Mode (2 speakers, left/right)
1. Power on **both** speakers
2. Make sure **neither** is paired to your phone yet (forget if needed)
3. Press the **Stereo button** on both speakers — a confirmation tone plays
4. LED flashes **white** on both
5. Pair your phone to the "master" speaker (whichever LED is solid)
6. Both speakers play — one left, one right channel

### Party Mode (up to 100 speakers)
1. Power on the **first** speaker and pair it to your phone normally
2. Power on additional speakers — leave them **unpaired**
3. Press the **Party button** on the first speaker, then on each additional
4. All speakers play the same audio simultaneously
5. To add more later, press Party on the new speaker while the original 
   is playing

### Multi-speaker troubleshooting
- **Second speaker doesn't join** — check both are on the same firmware 
  version (Settings in the app)
- **Audio cuts out on one speaker** — move speakers closer (within 10m) 
  and check for interference
- **Stereo mode not working** — must be exactly 2 speakers; 3+ speakers 
  use Party mode only
- **Party mode disconnects randomly** — reduce distance between 
  speakers, avoid walls or obstacles

### Firmware update
Multi-speaker features require both speakers to run the same firmware. 
To update:
1. Pair the speaker to your phone
2. Open the VoltNest app
3. Go to **My Devices → VoltBoom → Firmware**
4. Tap "Update" if an update is available
5. Repeat for each speaker

**Both speakers must be on the same firmware version.** Mixing firmware 
versions breaks Stereo and Party modes.

## Step 6: Audio Playing Through Phone Speaker

If audio plays through the phone instead of the VoltNest product, the 
product is **paired but not selected as the audio output**.

### iOS
1. Open the Control Center (swipe down from top-right)
2. Tap the **AirPlay icon** (a triangle with circles)
3. Select **VoltBuds Pro** or **VoltBoom**

### Android
1. Swipe down for quick settings
2. Tap **Media Output** (or similar)
3. Select **VoltBuds Pro** or **VoltBoom**

### Windows
1. Click the speaker icon in the taskbar
2. Select **VoltBuds Pro** or **VoltBoom** from the output list

### macOS
1. Click the speaker icon in the menu bar
2. Select **VoltBuds Pro** or **VoltBoom**

## Step 7: Only One Earbud Works

If only one earbud produces sound, the earbuds may have lost their 
left/right pairing.

### Fix
1. Place **both** earbuds in the case
2. Close the lid
3. Wait **10 seconds**
4. Open the lid — both earbuds should flash white
5. Remove both earbuds — audio should now be in both

### If still only one works
1. Return **both** earbuds to the case, keep lid **open**
2. Press and hold the case button for **15 seconds**
3. Earbuds reset — LED flashes red, then white
4. Forget "VoltBuds Pro" on your phone (Step 2)
5. Re-pair from scratch

### If one earbud still doesn't play
The non-working earbud may have a hardware fault. Test:

1. Use the non-working earbud **alone** (leave the other in the case)
2. If it still doesn't play audio, it may be defective
3. Contact support for a warranty claim — see 
   [Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty_policy.md)

## Step 8: Connection Drops or Cuts Out Frequently

Bluetooth audio requires a clear 2.4 GHz signal. Interference causes 
dropouts.

### Common causes and fixes

**Distance**
- VoltBuds Pro: keep within **10 meters** of the phone
- VoltBoom: keep within **30 meters** of the phone
- Walls, doors, and bodies reduce range significantly

**Wi-Fi interference**
- 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks compete with Bluetooth
- Try moving away from routers, or switch Wi-Fi to 5 GHz

**Microwave ovens**
- Microwaves emit 2.4 GHz and interfere during operation
- Move away from the kitchen while using the product

**USB 3.0 ports**
- USB 3.0 cables and hubs emit 2.4 GHz noise
- Move the phone or product away from USB 3.0 devices

**Crowded environments**
- Airports, cafes, and offices have many Bluetooth devices
- Interference is unavoidable — expect occasional glitches

### If drops persist
1. Forget and re-pair (Step 2)
2. Factory reset the product (Step 3)
3. Test with a different phone
4. If drops continue on multiple phones, contact support

## Step 9: Firmware Updates

Firmware updates fix known bugs and improve stability. Most "pairing" 
issues that survive a factory reset are fixed by firmware.

### How to update
1. Pair the device to your phone
2. Open the VoltNest app
3. Go to **My Devices**
4. Select the product
5. Tap **"Check for Updates"**
6. If an update is available, tap **"Install"**
7. Keep the device within 1 meter of the phone during update
8. Update takes 3–5 minutes — do not power off

### VoltBuds Pro current firmware
- Latest: **1.4.2** (as of January 2026)
- Fixes: multipoint switching, iOS 17 stability

### VoltBoom current firmware
- Latest: **2.1.0** (as of January 2026)
- Fixes: Party mode stability, multipoint conflicts, battery reporting

## Step 10: Test on Another Device

If the product still won't pair, test it on a **different phone** or 
tablet:

### If it works on another device
The issue is with your original phone. Try:
- Updating your phone's OS
- Factory resetting your phone's network settings
- Contacting your phone manufacturer (Bluetooth issues are phone-side)

### If it doesn't work on any device
The product may have a hardware fault. Contact support for warranty 
service — see [Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty_policy.md).

## What NOT to Do

### ❌ Don't pair through the VoltNest app
The app requires an active Bluetooth pairing first. Pair through your 
phone's system settings, not the app.

### ❌ Don't factory reset before trying forget-and-re-pair
Step 2 solves most issues. Step 3 (factory reset) is more drastic and 
requires re-pairing every device.

### ❌ Don't pair a VoltBoom to a VoltBuds Pro
They don't pair to each other. They each pair to your phone. Multi-speaker 
mode is only between multiple VoltBoom speakers.

### ❌ Don't leave the earbuds out of the case during a reset
Both earbuds must be in the case for the reset to work. One left out 
breaks the reset.

### ❌ Don't mix firmware versions in Stereo or Party mode
Both speakers must have the same firmware version. Update both before 
linking.

### ❌ Don't cover the earbuds with your hands
Your body blocks Bluetooth signal. If pairing fails while holding the 
earbuds, try again with them in the case.

### ❌ Don't use third-party Bluetooth apps
Only pair through your phone's system Bluetooth settings. Apps that 
"boost signal" or "manage Bluetooth" cause conflicts.

## For Support Agents

Internal notes for Tier 1 and Tier 2 agents handling Bluetooth issues.

### Triage Questions
- Which product? VoltBuds Pro or VoltBoom?
- What phone and OS version?
- What product firmware version?
- When did the issue start? First use, after update, or after working?
- Specific symptom (won't pair, one earbud only, disconnects, etc.)?
- Does it work with another phone?

### Tier 1 Scope
Tier 1 can resolve:
- Basic pairing guidance (Step 1)
- Forget-and-re-pair (Step 2)
- Audio output selection on phone (Step 6)
- One-earbud troubleshooting (Step 7)
- Interference guidance (Step 8)
- Firmware update guidance (Step 9)

### Tier 2 Escalation Criteria
Escalate to Tier 2 when:
- Customer completed Steps 1–10 and product still fails
- Product fails on **multiple devices** (likely hardware fault)
- Firmware update fails repeatedly
- Customer reports audio quality issues after successful pairing 
  (route to `audio_quality_issues.md`)
- Suspected hardware fault requiring warranty claim

### Warranty Routing
If the product is defective (won't pair on any device after factory 
reset + firmware update), route to warranty:

1. Confirm order ID and purchase date
2. Verify product is within 12-month warranty
3. Open warranty claim per [Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty_policy.md)

### Do NOT Tell the Customer
- Do not suggest third-party Bluetooth apps or "boosters"
- Do not ask them to reset their phone entirely
- Do not tell them the earbuds pair to each other (they pair to the 
  phone)
- Do not confirm a hardware fault without multi-device testing
