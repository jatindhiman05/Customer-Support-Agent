## What is GaN?

GaN (Gallium Nitride) is a semiconductor material that's more 
energy-efficient than traditional silicon. It lets us build chargers 
that are **smaller, cooler, and more efficient** at the same power 
output. Your VoltCharge 65W is roughly 40% smaller than an equivalent 
silicon charger.

## Common Questions

**Can it charge a MacBook Pro?**  
Yes for MacBook Pro 13" (Intel) and MacBook Air (M1/M2/M3). For 
MacBook Pro 14"/16" at full performance, you'll need a 96W+ charger — 
the VoltCharge 65W will charge them slowly but won't keep up under 
heavy load.

**Can I charge two laptops at once?**  
Yes — but at reduced power. Port 1 gets 45W, Port 2 gets 20W. That's 
enough for two MacBook Airs, but only slow-charging for high-power 
laptops.

**Does it come with a cable?**  
No. Cables are sold separately. You'll need a USB-C to USB-C cable or 
USB-C to Lightning cable, depending on your device.

**Can I use it internationally?**  
Yes. Input voltage is 100–240V, so it works in any country. You'll 
just need a plug adapter for non-US outlets (the charger itself is 
not a travel adapter).

**Does it work with iPhone?**  
Yes. For iPhone 15/16, use a USB-C to USB-C cable for fast charging. 
For iPhone 12–14, use a USB-C to Lightning cable (sold separately).

**Will it charge my phone as fast as the original charger?**  
For most phones, yes — faster. The 65W output supports USB-PD 3.0, 
PPS, and QC 4.0+, which cover fast-charging standards for iPhone, 
Samsung, Google, and most Android phones.

**Is it safe to leave plugged in overnight?**  
Yes. The charger has over-current, over-voltage, and over-temperature 
protection. It will stop charging when your device is full.

**Can I use it with a power strip or extension cord?**  
Yes, but the power strip must support at least 100W total. Cheap power 
strips may not deliver full power.

**Does it get hot?**  
It gets warm during high-power charging, which is normal. The GaN 
design runs cooler than silicon chargers. If it gets too hot to touch, 
stop using it and contact support.

**Does it work with VoltBank 20K power bank?**  
Yes. Recharging VoltBank 20K from 0–100% takes about **2 hours** with 
the VoltCharge 65W (using a 100W-rated USB-C cable).

**What cable should I buy?**  
For full 65W output, use a **100W-rated USB-C to USB-C cable**. Standard 
60W cables will cap charging at 60W. We don't currently sell cables — 
any certified 100W cable from Amazon or Best Buy works.

**Is it compatible with the Nintendo Switch?**  
Yes — works in both handheld and docked modes.

## Care and Maintenance

- Keep away from water and moisture
- Do not use in high-humidity environments (bathrooms, near pools)
- Unplug during electrical storms
- Do not wrap the cable tightly around the charger — this can damage 
  internal wiring
- Clean with a dry cloth only
