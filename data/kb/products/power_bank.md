## Common Questions

**Can it charge a laptop?**  
Yes — up to 65W via USB-C 1. This is enough for MacBook Air, MacBook 
Pro 13" (Intel), Dell XPS 13, and similar laptops. For MacBook Pro 
14"/16" or gaming laptops, output is capped and charging will be slow.

**How many times can it charge my phone?**  
About 4 full charges for iPhone 15, or 3 for a Pro Max. See the 
"Charging Capacity" table above for more devices.

**How long does it take to recharge?**  
About 2 hours with the VoltCharge 65W (recommended). About 4 hours 
with a 30W charger. Under 30W chargers take 6+ hours.

**Does it come with a charger?**  
No — the box includes a USB-C cable but no wall charger. You can use 
any USB-C charger you already own, or the VoltCharge 65W (sold 
separately, $39.99).

**Can I charge it and charge my phone at the same time?**  
Yes — pass-through charging is supported. The bank recharges while 
simultaneously outputting power to your device. Note that this slows 
the bank's recharge.

**Can I take it on a plane?**  
Yes — in your carry-on bag. **Not allowed in checked luggage** (FAA 
and international rules). At 74 Wh, it's under the 100 Wh limit for 
carry-on batteries.

**How do I know how much battery is left?**  
VoltBank 20K has a **digital LED display** that shows the exact 
percentage (e.g., "87%"). No guessing with bars or dots.

**Can I use it while it's charging my laptop?**  
Yes — pass-through charging works. But note that high-power laptops 
may not charge quickly while the bank is also recharging.

**Does it work with iPhone?**  
Yes. For iPhone 15/16, use the included USB-C cable. For iPhone 
12–14, you'll need a USB-C to Lightning cable (sold separately).

**Is it waterproof?**  
No. VoltBank 20K is **not water-resistant**. Keep it dry. Do not use 
near water, in rain, or in high-humidity environments.

**Does it overheat?**  
Under normal use, it stays warm (not hot). Built-in temperature 
protection will reduce output or pause charging if it gets too warm. 
If it feels very hot to the touch, stop using it and contact support.

**Can I ship it internationally?**  
**No.** VoltBank 20K cannot be shipped internationally due to lithium 
battery regulations. It ships to US addresses only.

**What's the lifespan of the battery?**  
500+ full charge cycles. Under typical use (recharging 2–3 times per 
week), that's roughly **3–4 years** before capacity noticeably declines.

**Does it come in other colors?**  
No — currently available in Midnight Black only.

## Battery Care

To maximize the lifespan of your VoltBank 20K:

- **Avoid full discharges** — recharge at 20–30% remaining
- **Avoid full charges** — unplugging at 80–90% reduces cell stress
- **Store at 50–60% charge** if not using for weeks
- **Avoid extreme temperatures** (below 0°C / 32°F or above 45°C / 113°F)
- **Recharge every 3 months** if unused for long periods
- **Do not puncture, crush, or disassemble** — lithium batteries are 
  dangerous if damaged

**Do not** leave it in a hot car. High heat permanently damages 
lithium-ion cells.
