## Common Questions

**Is the VoltBoom really waterproof?**  
Yes. It has an **IP67** rating, which means it's fully dust-proof and 
can be submerged in up to 1 meter of water for up to 30 minutes. You 
can take it to the pool, beach, or shower. It floats.

**Can I take it swimming?**  
It floats, but we don't recommend leaving it in water for extended 
periods. Submersion is fine for accidental drops or splashes.

**How loud is it?**  
20W RMS output with dual passive radiators. It's loud enough for a 
small outdoor gathering (up to ~50 people). For larger spaces, pair 
multiple VoltBoom speakers.

**Can I connect two speakers together?**  
Yes. Two ways:
- **Stereo mode**: Pair 2 speakers for true left/right stereo
- **Party mode**: Link up to 100 speakers together for a single 
  synchronized stream

**How many speakers can I link?**  
Up to **100 VoltBoom speakers** in Party mode.

**How long does the battery last?**  
24 hours at 50% volume. At maximum volume, approximately 8 hours. 
Charging takes 2.5 hours via USB-C.

**Can I use it while charging?**  
Yes. You can play music while the speaker is plugged in.

**Does it have a microphone for calls?**  
Yes — a built-in microphone lets you take phone calls or join video 
meetings. The mic is on the top of the speaker.

**Can I use it with a wired device?**  
Yes. There's a 3.5 mm aux input for devices without Bluetooth (older 
MP3 players, some turntables, etc.).

**Does it work with Alexa or Google Home?**  
It works with Siri and Google Assistant voice commands via your paired 
device, but it does not have Alexa built in.

**Can I charge my phone from the speaker?**  
No. The USB-C port on VoltBoom is for charging the speaker only. It 
does not output power to other devices.

**Is the strap removable?**  
Yes. The adjustable carry strap clips on and off. It also has a 
standard 1/4"-20 tripod mount on the bottom for mounting on stands.

**What's the difference between VoltBoom and VoltBuds Pro?**  
The VoltBuds Pro ($79.99) are personal wireless earbuds for one 
listener. The VoltBoom ($59.99) is a portable speaker for shared 
listening. Different use cases.

## Care and Maintenance

To keep your VoltBoom working well:

- Rinse with fresh water after exposure to saltwater or pool water
- Dry the speaker before charging (wet USB-C port can short)
- Wipe with a soft cloth — do not use solvents or abrasives
- Store in a dry place when not in use
- Do not leave in direct sunlight for extended periods (can affect 
  the battery)
- Recharge the battery every 3 months if not in use
