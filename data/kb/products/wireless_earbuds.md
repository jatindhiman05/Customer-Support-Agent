## Sound Profile

VoltBuds Pro are tuned for a **balanced, natural sound** with:

- **Punchy but controlled bass** — you feel it, but it doesn't drown 
  out vocals
- **Clear midrange** — vocals and podcasts sound full and forward
- **Crisp highs** — detailed without being harsh or fatiguing
- **Wide soundstage** — instruments feel spaced, not cramped

For **bass-heavy** listeners, we recommend using your music app's EQ 
(Spotify, Apple Music). For **neutral** listeners, they sound great 
out of the box.

**Note**: VoltBuds Pro do **not** have active noise cancellation (ANC). 
They use passive noise isolation through the ear tip seal. For ANC, 
wait for our planned VoltBuds Pro+ (coming 2026).

## Common Questions

**Does the VoltBuds Pro have active noise cancellation (ANC)?**  
No. It has **passive noise isolation** only, which reduces ambient noise 
through the ear tip seal but does not actively cancel sound. For ANC, 
see the planned VoltBuds Pro+ (coming 2026).

**Can I use one earbud at a time?**  
Yes. Either the left or right earbud works independently for calls and 
music. This is useful for phone calls or when you need to stay aware 
of your surroundings.

**Is it good for the gym?**  
Yes. The IPX5 rating handles sweat and light rain. Do **not** submerge 
in water or wear in the shower.

**How long does the battery last on a single charge?**  
8 hours of continuous playback. The charging case provides 3 additional 
full charges, totaling **32 hours** before you need a wall outlet.

**How fast does it charge?**  
A 10-minute quick charge provides 2 hours of playback. A full charge 
of the earbuds takes 90 minutes; the case takes 2 hours.

**Can I connect it to two devices at once?**  
Yes — multipoint pairing supports **2 devices simultaneously**. 
For example, you can have it paired to your phone and laptop at the 
same time, switching automatically when a call comes in.

**Does it work with an iPhone?**  
Yes, fully compatible with iOS 12 and above.

**Can I use it for video calls (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet)?**  
Yes. The dual-mic ENC system is optimized for voice calls and video 
conferencing.

**Is it compatible with wireless charging?**  
The earbuds themselves are not wirelessly charged. The charging case 
supports Qi wireless charging — just place it on any Qi pad.

**Can I take it in the shower or swimming?**  
No. IPX5 protects against splashes, sweat, and light rain — not 
immersion. For swimming, we recommend keeping the earbuds away from 
water entirely.

**What's the difference between VoltBuds Pro and VoltBuds?**  
The VoltBuds Pro ($79.99) has dual-mic ENC, multipoint pairing, and 
a wireless-charging case. The standard VoltBuds ($49.99) has a single 
mic and wired charging. The Pro is the better choice for calls and 
multitasking.

**Does it come with a warranty?**  
Yes — 12 months against manufacturing defects. See the 
[Warranty Policy](../policies/warranty_policy.md) for details.

**How do I update the firmware?**  
1. Pair the earbuds to your phone
2. Open the VoltNest app
3. Go to **My Devices → VoltBuds Pro → Check for Updates**
4. Keep the earbuds within 1 meter of the phone during the update
5. Update takes 3–5 minutes

Current firmware: **1.4.2**

**How do I reset the earbuds?**  
Place both earbuds in the case, keep the lid open, and press and hold 
the button on the back of the case for 15 seconds. The LED will flash 
red, then white. See 
[Bluetooth Pairing Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md) 
for details.

**Does it work with the Nintendo Switch?**  
Yes — works with the Switch in both handheld and docked modes.

**Is there a warranty extension available?**  
No. VoltNest does not offer extended warranty options. All products 
have a standard 12-month warranty.

**Can I replace just one earbud if I lose it?**  
Replacement earbuds are not sold separately. If you lose one, you'll 
need to purchase a new pair. Consider adding a Find My Device app for 
future protection.

## Care and Maintenance

To keep your VoltBuds Pro working well:

- Clean ear tips weekly with a dry, soft cloth
- Replace ear tips every 3–6 months for hygiene and sound quality
- Store in the charging case when not in use
- Avoid extreme temperatures (below -10°C / 14°F or above 45°C / 113°F)
- Do not use alcohol or abrasive cleaners on the earbuds
- Wipe charging contacts (small gold dots) with a dry cotton swab if 
  they look dirty
