## Common Questions

**Will it fit my Apple Watch?**  
Yes — VoltFit works with all Apple Watch generations. The band 
includes a custom adapter that connects to Apple's proprietary 
connector. Select **22 mm** at checkout.

**How do I know which size to order?**  
Measure your wrist with a tape measure:
- **S/M**: 130–180 mm (5.1"–7.1") — most women and smaller wrists
- **M/L**: 150–210 mm (5.9"–8.3") — most men and larger wrists

If you're between sizes, order M/L — the band can be adjusted tighter.

**Can I swim with it?**  
Yes. Both silicone and nylon bands are fully waterproof. The steel 
buckle won't rust. Rinse with fresh water after swimming in saltwater 
or pools.

**Is it comfortable for all-day wear?**  
Yes. The silicone band is soft and flexible, and the nylon weave 
breathes. For hot climates or heavy workouts, we recommend the **nylon 
weave** version.

**Which is better, silicone or nylon?**  
- **Silicone**: more durable, easier to clean, better for swimming 
  and sweaty workouts
- **Nylon weave**: more breathable, lighter, dries slower when wet, 
  better for hot climates and casual wear

**Does it cause skin irritation?**  
Very rarely. VoltFit is **hypoallergenic** — no latex, and the 
stainless steel buckle is nickel-free. If you have very sensitive 
skin, the **nylon version** is your best bet.

**Will it fit my Samsung Galaxy Watch?**  
Yes — for Galaxy Watch 4, 5, and 6 (all sizes), select **20 mm**.

**How do I swap the band?**  
VoltFit uses standard quick-release spring bars. Slide the small lever 
on the underside of the band, and it pops off. No tools needed. Takes 
about 10 seconds.

**Does it fit a Garmin?**  
Yes — most Garmin watches use 22 mm spring bars. Select **22 mm**. 
If your Garmin has an integrated band (not removable), VoltFit will 
not fit.

**Can I use it for formal occasions?**  
VoltFit is a sport band. It works with casual and athletic outfits. 
For formal occasions, we don't currently offer a leather or metal 
band.

**Does the color fade over time?**  
The **Midnight Black** and **Forest Green** colors resist fading. The 
**Ocean Blue** and **Rose Gold** may show slight color wear after 
12+ months of heavy use, especially with frequent swimming.

**Is it compatible with Fitbit?**  
Only with Fitbit Versa and Sense models, which use 20 mm bands. 
**Not compatible** with Fitbit Charge or Inspire (proprietary 
connectors).

**Does the band come with the watch?**  
No — VoltFit is a **replacement band only**. The smartwatch itself is 
not included.

**Is the buckle metal?**  
Yes. Silicone bands use a **stainless steel** buckle. Nylon bands use 
an **aluminum** buckle. Both are corrosion-resistant.

**Does it work with the Apple Watch Ultra?**  
Yes, but note: the Ultra has a different lug design (49 mm case). 
VoltFit still fits, but the band width may look slightly narrower 
than the Ultra's native bands. Functionally it works perfectly.

**Can I wear it in the shower?**  
Yes. Both materials are water-safe and won't degrade from soap or 
shampoo.

## Care and Maintenance

### Silicone bands
- Wipe daily with a soft cloth
- For deep cleaning, wash with mild soap and warm water, then air dry
- Avoid prolonged contact with sunscreen, lotion, or oils (may discolor)
- Do not use alcohol or acetone

### Nylon weave bands
- Hand-wash with mild soap and warm water
- Air dry completely before wearing (nylon holds moisture)
- Avoid machine washing (can damage the weave)
- Replace if the weave starts to fray after extended use
