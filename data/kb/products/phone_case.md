## Common Questions

**Will it work with wireless charging?**  
Yes. VoltShield is thin enough (1.8 mm) to allow full-speed wireless 
charging without removing the case. Works with all Qi chargers.

**Is it MagSafe compatible?**  
Yes. Built-in magnet array aligns with MagSafe accessories — wallets, 
stands, car mounts, and chargers. Magnet strength is rated at 12N 
(equivalent to Apple's official MagSafe cases).

**How much drop protection does it offer?**  
Up to 3 meters (10 feet) on flat surfaces. Tested to MIL-STD-810G 
(military drop test standard). This covers typical drops from pockets, 
tables, and standing height.

**Does it protect the camera?**  
Yes. There's a **2 mm raised bezel** around the camera module, so the 
lens never touches flat surfaces when the phone is placed face-down.

**Does it protect the screen?**  
Yes. There's a **1.5 mm raised lip** around the front edges, protecting 
the screen from face-down drops. We still recommend a screen protector 
for full protection.

**Does it yellow over time?**  
The **Midnight Black** and **Desert Tan** colors won't yellow. The 
**Crystal Clear** version has an anti-yellowing coating that resists 
discoloration for **12+ months**. Some yellowing may occur after that, 
which is normal for clear TPU.

**Is it slippery?**  
No. The matte finish provides good grip. It's noticeably less slippery 
than a bare iPhone.

**Does it support the Apple Watch?**  
No — VoltShield is a phone case only. We don't currently sell Apple 
Watch cases.

**Is it waterproof?**  
No. VoltShield is water-resistant to splashes but not waterproof. Do 
not submerge your phone in water with or without the case.

**Does it add much bulk?**  
VoltShield adds 1.8 mm thickness and 32 g weight. It's one of the 
slimmest protective cases available — noticeably thinner than OtterBox 
or Lifeproof options.

**Can I use it with a screen protector?**  
Yes. The 1.5 mm raised lip accommodates most screen protectors, 
including tempered glass. We recommend a screen protector thickness 
of 0.33 mm or less.

**Does it come with a screen protector?**  
No — the case only. Screen protectors are sold separately.

**Will it fit in my car mount?**  
Yes, if the mount is MagSafe or a universal clamp. The MagSafe magnet 
array works with all MagSafe car mounts.

**Is it antimicrobial?**  
Yes. The silver-ion coating on the surface inhibits bacterial growth 
by up to 99.9%. It's built into the material and does not wash off.

**What if my phone model isn't listed?**  
We only manufacture VoltShield for the models listed above. If your 
phone isn't listed, we don't currently have a case for it. Check back 
— we add new models 1–2 times per year.

## Care and Maintenance

To keep your VoltShield looking good:

- Wipe with a soft, damp cloth weekly
- For deeper cleaning, use mild soap and water — dry immediately
- Do not use alcohol, acetone, or abrasive cleaners (damages the finish)
- Remove the case monthly to clean the phone and case interior
- Avoid leaving in direct sunlight for extended periods
