# Cancellation Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

This policy explains when and how customers can cancel orders placed on 
voltnest.com, what happens after an order ships, and how refunds are 
handled for cancelled orders. It applies to all orders worldwide.

**Note**: Cancellation is different from a return. Cancellation happens 
**before** delivery; returns happen **after**. If your order has already 
been delivered, see the [Return & Refund Policy](./return_refund_policy.md).

## Cancellation Window

Customers can cancel an order **any time before it ships**. 

The critical cutoff is **2:00 PM EST** on the day the order was placed:

| Order Placed | Can Cancel Freely? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Before 2:00 PM EST, Mon–Fri | ✅ Yes — same day | Order may ship same day |
| After 2:00 PM EST, Mon–Fri | ⚠️ Attempt only | Order may already be packed |
| Saturday or Sunday | ✅ Yes — until Monday 2 PM | Ships Monday |
| US holiday | ✅ Yes — until next business day 2 PM | Ships next business day |

**Once an order ships**, it can no longer be cancelled. It must be 
returned after delivery instead.

## How to Cancel an Order

### Self-Service Cancellation

1. Log in to your VoltNest account
2. Go to **Orders → Select order → "Cancel Order"**
3. Confirm the cancellation

You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email immediately.

**Self-service cancellation is only available if the order has not yet 
entered "Packed" status.** Once packed, you must contact support.

### Cancellation via Support

If the self-service option is unavailable (order is packed or shipped):

1. Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Cancel Order"**
2. Include your **order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
3. We respond within **4 business hours**

If the order has not yet been picked up by the carrier, we can 
intercept it. If it has shipped, we'll guide you through the return 
process instead.

## Refund Timeline for Cancellations

Cancelled orders are refunded in full — including any shipping fees paid.

| Cancellation Stage | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Before packing | Within 1 business day |
| After packing, before carrier pickup | Within 2 business days |
| After carrier pickup (intercept) | 5–7 business days after carrier confirms |

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method only**. See the 
[Return & Refund Policy](./return_refund_policy.md) for the full 
payment-method-specific refund timeline.

## What Can't Be Cancelled

Some orders cannot be cancelled once placed:

- **Personalized or custom-engraved items** — production begins immediately
- **Pre-orders** — see the Pre-Order section below
- **Orders already shipped** — must be returned after delivery
- **Gift cards** — non-refundable once issued
- **Digital downloads** — non-refundable once delivered

## Partial Cancellations

For orders with **multiple items**, you can cancel individual items 
before the order ships:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Orders → Select order → "Modify Order"**
3. Select the items you want to remove

**Shipping fees are recalculated** based on the remaining items. If the 
new order total falls below the free shipping threshold ($35), standard 
shipping ($4.99) is added.

**Once any item in an order ships**, the entire order is locked. Partial 
cancellations are no longer possible — use the return process instead.

## Cancellation During Peak Seasons

During the following high-volume periods, our cancellation window may 
be shorter because orders move to packing faster:

- Black Friday through Cyber Monday
- December 15 – December 26
- The week of July 4
- The week of Thanksgiving

During these periods, the self-service cancellation window may close 
in **under 30 minutes**. Contact support immediately if you need to 
cancel during peak season.

## Pre-Orders

Pre-orders (products not yet released) can be cancelled:

- **Any time before the release date** — full refund
- **On the release date, before 2:00 PM EST** — full refund
- **After the release date ships** — becomes a standard return

Pre-order cancellations are always free and instant.

## Subscription Cancellations

VoltNest does not currently offer subscription products. All purchases 
are one-time. If you believe you have a recurring charge from VoltNest, 
contact support@voltnest.com immediately — this may indicate fraud.

## Canceled by VoltNest

In rare cases, VoltNest may cancel an order on our end:

- **Out of stock** — item sold out between order and packing
- **Pricing error** — order placed at an incorrect price
- **Suspected fraud** — flagged by our payment processor
- **Shipping restriction** — destination not supported

If we cancel your order:

1. You receive an email within **24 hours** explaining the reason
2. A **full refund** is issued immediately (1 business day)
3. Where possible, we offer a **10% discount code** for your next order

We apologize for any inconvenience. Cancellations by VoltNest are rare 
(fewer than 0.5% of orders).

## Fraud-Related Cancellations

If your order is cancelled due to a suspected fraud alert:

1. We email you with next steps
2. You may be asked to verify your identity or payment method
3. Once verified, we can re-process the order
4. Your information is handled per our [Privacy Policy](./privacy_policy.md)

Fraud checks protect both you and VoltNest. Thank you for your patience.

## Refused Delivery

If you refuse delivery of a package from the carrier:

- The package returns to our Memphis distribution center
- We process the return once received (5–7 business days after arrival)
- Shipping fees are **not refunded** for refused deliveries
- A **15% restocking fee** may apply for refused international deliveries

If you don't want the order, cancel it before shipping instead of 
refusing delivery — it saves everyone time and cost.

## Cancellation vs. Return

| | Cancellation | Return |
|---|---|---|
| **When** | Before shipping | After delivery |
| **Refund** | Full, including shipping | Full, minus return shipping |
| **Time to refund** | 1–2 business days | 10–17 business days |
| **Return shipping cost** | $0 | $6.99 (US) unless our error |
| **Process** | Account → Cancel | Account → Return + RMA |

**Rule of thumb**: If the package hasn't shipped, cancel. If it has, 
return it.

## How Long Refunds Take

After cancellation:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit/Debit card | 5–7 business days |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–7 business days |
| Klarna / Afterpay | Adjusted against installments |
| Gift card | Immediate (new gift card issued) |

VoltNest issues refunds within 1–2 business days of cancellation 
approval. Remaining time depends on your bank or payment provider.
