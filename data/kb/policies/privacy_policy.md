# Privacy Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information VoltNest collects, 
how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over your 
data. It applies to all customers and visitors of voltnest.com, 
regardless of location.

**Last updated**: January 15, 2026.  
**Effective date**: January 15, 2026.

## Information We Collect

VoltNest collects information in four categories.

### 1. Information You Provide

- **Account details**: name, email address, phone number, password
- **Shipping address**: street, city, state/province, postal code, country
- **Payment details**: card type, last 4 digits, billing address (we never 
  store full card numbers — see "Third-Party Processors")
- **Order content**: items purchased, order totals, order IDs
- **Communications**: emails, chat transcripts, support tickets, reviews
- **Optional profile data**: birthday (for birthday offers), gender 
  (optional), marketing preferences

### 2. Information Collected Automatically

- **Device data**: browser type, operating system, device model
- **Log data**: IP address, approximate location (city-level), pages viewed, 
  time on page, referring URL
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: see the "Cookies" section below

### 3. Information from Third Parties

- **Payment processors** (Stripe, PayPal): transaction confirmations and 
  fraud signals
- **Shipping carriers** (UPS, USPS, FedEx, DHL): delivery confirmations 
  and address validation
- **Analytics providers** (Google Analytics): aggregated usage data
- **Marketing partners**: email engagement data (opens, clicks)

### 4. Information We Do Not Collect

- Full credit card numbers (handled by Stripe)
- Social Security numbers or government IDs
- Biometric data
- Data from children under 13 (we do not knowingly collect from minors)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Fulfill orders**: process payments, ship products, provide tracking
- **Provide support**: respond to questions, resolve disputes, process 
  returns and warranty claims
- **Improve our products**: analyze aggregate usage patterns to fix bugs 
  and design better products
- **Personalize your experience**: recommend products, remember preferences
- **Communicate**: order updates, shipping confirmations, service emails
- **Marketing** (only with consent): promotional emails, offers, newsletters
- **Prevent fraud**: detect suspicious activity, verify identity
- **Comply with law**: tax reporting, legal requests, regulatory requirements

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

## Third-Party Processors

VoltNest shares data with the following trusted processors, each bound 
by their own privacy agreements and contracts with us:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Stripe** | Payment processing | Name, email, billing address, order total |
| **PayPal** | Alternative payments | Name, email, order total |
| **UPS, USPS, FedEx, DHL** | Shipping & tracking | Name, shipping address, phone |
| **ShipStation** | Shipping orchestration | Order details, addresses |
| **Google Analytics** | Usage analytics | IP (anonymized), pages viewed |
| **Klaviyo** | Email marketing (opt-in) | Email, order history |
| **Zendesk** | Support ticketing | Email, chat history, order IDs |
| **AWS** | Cloud infrastructure | All data (encrypted at rest) |

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information** to 
advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party.

## Cookies and Tracking

VoltNest uses cookies and similar technologies for:

- **Essential cookies**: cart, login session, CSRF protection (cannot be 
  disabled)
- **Performance cookies**: page speed, error tracking
- **Analytics cookies**: Google Analytics (aggregate behavior)
- **Marketing cookies**: ad retargeting (opt-in only)

### Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

- **Browser settings**: block or delete cookies (may break site features)
- **Our cookie banner**: adjust categories on your first visit
- **Opt-out links**: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

For EU/UK visitors, non-essential cookies require explicit consent per 
GDPR.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes 
described above.

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Account information | Until account deletion + 30 days |
| Order history | 7 years (tax & legal requirements) |
| Support chat transcripts | 24 months |
| Marketing preferences | Until opt-out + 30 days |
| Analytics (aggregate) | 26 months |
| Fraud signals | 5 years |
| Cookie consent records | 3 years |

Order records are kept for 7 years to comply with US tax and consumer 
protection laws, even after account deletion. Chat transcripts are kept 
for 24 months to improve support quality and resolve disputes.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### All Customers
- **Access**: request a copy of your personal data
- **Correct**: update inaccurate information
- **Delete**: request deletion of your data (with legal exceptions)
- **Opt out of marketing**: unsubscribe at any time

### EU/UK Customers (GDPR)
- **Data portability**: receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Restrict processing**: limit how we use your data
- **Object**: object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Withdraw consent**: for consent-based processing at any time
- **Lodge a complaint**: with your local data protection authority

### California Customers (CCPA/CPRA)
- **Know**: what personal information we collect and share
- **Delete**: request deletion of personal information
- **Correct**: request correction of inaccurate information
- **Opt out of "sale"**: we do not sell personal information, but you may 
  still submit a request
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not discriminate for exercising rights

### How to Exercise Your Rights

Email **privacy@voltnest.com** with:

- Your name
- The email on your account
- The right you want to exercise
- Any relevant details

We respond within **30 days** (or 45 days for complex requests). 
Verification of identity may be required.

## Security

We protect your information with:

- **Encryption in transit**: HTTPS/TLS 1.3 for all site traffic
- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256 for stored data
- **PCI-DSS compliance**: for all payment handling
- **Access controls**: least-privilege access for employees
- **Regular audits**: quarterly security reviews
- **Bug bounty**: paid program for security researchers

**No system is 100% secure.** If we discover a data breach affecting 
your information, we will notify you within **72 hours** of confirmation, 
as required by law.

## Children's Privacy

VoltNest does not knowingly collect personal information from children 
under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, email 
privacy@voltnest.com and we will delete it promptly.

## International Data Transfers

VoltNest is based in the United States. Your data may be transferred 
to and processed in the US and other countries where our service 
providers operate. For EU/UK customers, we use **Standard Contractual 
Clauses** approved by the European Commission to protect your data 
during transfer.
