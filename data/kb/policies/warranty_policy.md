# Warranty Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Every VoltNest product is covered by a **12-month limited warranty** 
against manufacturing defects. This policy explains what's covered, 
what's not, how to file a claim, and how claims are resolved.

**For returns due to change of mind, see the [Return & Refund Policy](./return_refund_policy.md) instead.** This warranty covers defects — 
not preferences.

## Warranty Coverage

Every product sold on voltnest.com includes a 12-month limited warranty 
that starts on the **delivery date** confirmed by the carrier.

| Product | Warranty Period |
|---|---|
| VoltBuds Pro Wireless Earbuds | 12 months |
| VoltBoom Bluetooth Speaker | 12 months |
| VoltCharge 65W USB-C Charger | 12 months |
| VoltBank 20K Power Bank | 12 months |
| VoltShield Phone Case | 12 months |
| VoltFit Smartwatch Band | 12 months |

The warranty is **non-transferable**. It applies to the original 
purchaser only and cannot be extended, renewed, or gifted.

## What's Covered

The warranty covers **manufacturing defects** that appear during normal 
use, including:

- Battery failure not caused by misuse or physical damage
- Charging failures due to internal component faults
- Bluetooth or wireless connectivity failures
- Speaker driver failure (distortion, no sound)
- Buttons, ports, or connectors that stop working
- Materials that separate, crack, or fail under normal use
- Firmware or software faults that prevent normal operation

**Key principle**: If the product fails because of how it was made, we 
cover it. If it fails because of how it was used, we don't.

## What's Not Covered

The warranty does **not** cover:

### Damage from misuse or accidents
- Drops, impacts, or crushed items
- Liquid damage beyond the product's IP rating
- Damage from heat, fire, or extreme temperatures
- Bent connectors, frayed cables, or broken ports from force

### Normal wear and tear
- Cosmetic scratches, scuffs, or fading
- Ear tip wear or deformation
- Battery capacity degradation under 20% of original spec
- Color fading on fabric or silicone parts

### Loss and theft
- Lost or stolen items
- Items damaged after being lost

### Unauthorized modifications
- Products disassembled or repaired by unauthorized parties
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers
- Products modified with non-VoltNest parts or accessories

### Purchases outside authorized channels
- Products bought from third-party marketplaces (eBay, Amazon 
  resellers, etc.)
- Second-hand or refurbished products not sold directly by VoltNest
- Counterfeit or "gray market" products

## How to File a Warranty Claim

Filing a claim takes 3 steps. Most claims are resolved within 
**5 business days**.

### Step 1: Contact Support
Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Warranty Claim"** and include:

- Your **order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- The **product name or SKU**
- A **description of the issue** — what stopped working, when, and how
- **Photos or video** showing the defect clearly

Claims without photos or video may take longer to process.

### Step 2: Diagnostic Review
Our support team reviews the claim and may ask follow-up questions to 
confirm the defect is covered. Common follow-ups:

- Has the product been dropped or exposed to liquid?
- Has it been charged with the original cable?
- Has it been disassembled in any way?

Most diagnostics complete in **1–2 business days**.

### Step 3: Resolution
Once approved, we resolve the claim using the **repair-first** approach 
described below.

## Repair-First Resolution

Consistent with our [Mission & Values](../company/mission_values.md), 
VoltNest uses a **repair-first approach** to warranty claims. This is 
better for the planet and often faster for you.

The resolution order is:

1. **Remote fix** — We may send instructions or firmware to fix the issue 
   without needing the product back
2. **Repair** — If repairable, we repair the product and ship it back 
   at our cost
3. **Replacement** — If not repairable, we send a replacement (refurbished 
   if available, new otherwise)
4. **Refund** — If neither repair nor replacement is possible, we issue 
   a full refund

You'll always be told which resolution applies **before** you ship 
anything back.

## Shipping for Warranty Claims

- **VoltNest covers shipping** both ways for valid warranty claims
- A prepaid shipping label is provided after the claim is approved
- Do **not** ship the product before receiving a prepaid label
- Products shipped without authorization may be refused

For **international warranty claims**, VoltNest reimburses reasonable 
return shipping costs (up to $30 USD) upon approval.

## Replacement Products

Replacement products may be:

- **Refurbished** — fully tested, restored to like-new condition, and 
  covered by a fresh 90-day warranty
- **New** — identical new product, covered by the remainder of the 
  original warranty or 12 months, whichever is longer

If your product is discontinued, we offer either:

- The closest available equivalent
- A full refund of the original purchase price

## Warranty After Replacement

When a product is replaced under warranty:

- The **original warranty continues** if the replacement is new
- The **warranty resets to 90 days** if the replacement is refurbished
- The **clock does not reset** to 12 months

This prevents the warranty from being extended indefinitely through 
repeated claims.

## Extended Warranty

VoltNest does **not** currently offer extended warranty options. Our 
12-month coverage is the standard for all products.

We do not partner with third-party warranty providers. If another 
company contacts you about extending VoltNest coverage, it is not 
authorized and should be ignored.

## Warranty vs. Return

These are two separate processes. Confusing them is the most common 
source of support tickets.

| | Return | Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| **Reason** | Changed mind, didn't like it | Defective, broken |
| **Window** | 30 days from delivery | 12 months from delivery |
| **Shipping cost** | Customer pays (unless our error) | VoltNest pays |
| **Resolution** | Refund | Repair → replace → refund |
| **Condition required** | Unused, original packaging | Any — defect is the point |

**Rule of thumb**: If it worked when you got it and stopped working 
later, it's a warranty claim. If you simply don't want it, it's a return.

## Extended Protection for Damaged Deliveries

If your product arrives **damaged in transit** (visible box damage, 
physically broken on arrival), that's not a warranty claim — it's a 
shipping claim. Report it within **48 hours** of delivery and see our 
[Shipping Policy](./shipping_policy.md) for the process.

## Disputes

If your warranty claim is denied and you believe the decision is wrong:

1. Reply to the warranty email and request a **supervisor review**
2. Your case is escalated to a support manager within 1 business day
3. The manager reviews the full claim and photos/video
4. You receive a written decision within 3 business days

We don't always get it right the first time, but we always take a 
second look when asked.
