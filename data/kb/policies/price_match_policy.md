# Price Match Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

If you find a lower price on an identical VoltNest product from an 
approved competitor within 14 days of your purchase, we'll refund the 
difference. This policy explains what qualifies, what doesn't, and how 
to submit a price match request.

## Price Match Window

Price match requests must be submitted within **14 calendar days** of 
your **delivery date** — not the order date.

**Important**: This window is separate from our 30-day return window. 
You can request a price match on day 12 and still return the item on 
day 25 if you change your mind.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a price match, **all** of the following must be true:

- The product is **identical** (same SKU, color, size, and model)
- The competitor is on our **approved retailer list** (below)
- The competitor's price is **currently live and available**
- The item is **in stock** at the competitor
- The price is a **standard retail price** (not a promotional one — see exclusions)
- You purchased the item **directly from voltnest.com**

## Approved Competitors

Price matching applies to these retailers only:

- Amazon (sold and shipped by Amazon, not third-party sellers)
- Best Buy
- Walmart (sold and shipped by Walmart, not marketplace)
- Target
- B&H Photo Video
- Newegg (sold and shipped by Newegg)

**Not approved**: eBay, AliExpress, Temu, Wish, Facebook Marketplace, 
Craigslist, or any third-party marketplace seller on any platform.

## What Doesn't Qualify

The following do **not** qualify for a price match:

### Promotional prices
- Flash sales or lightning deals
- Black Friday, Cyber Monday, or Prime Day prices
- Clearance, closeout, or "final sale" items
- Bundle deals (e.g., "buy 2 get 1 free")
- Prices that require a membership or coupon
- Prices available only to new customers

### Non-identical products
- Different model numbers, even if similar
- Refurbished, open-box, or used items
- International versions or gray-market imports
- Bundled items (e.g., case + earbuds package)
- Older or newer generation SKUs

### Non-purchase scenarios
- Prices seen before you placed your order (buy-then-see doesn't count)
- Prices seen after the 14-day window
- Prices you negotiated elsewhere
- Prices from a personal or employee discount

## How to Submit a Price Match Request

### Step 1: Gather Evidence
Take a **screenshot** of the competitor's page showing:

- The product name and model
- The current price
- The URL (visible in the browser bar)
- The date and time (system clock visible)

**Photo evidence alone is not accepted.** It must be a screen capture of 
the live page.

### Step 2: Contact Support
Email support@voltnest.com with subject **"Price Match Request"** and include:

- Your **order ID** (format: `ORD-XXXXXX`)
- A **link** to the competitor's product page
- The **screenshot** as an attachment
- The **price you paid** and the **price you found**

### Step 3: Review
Our team reviews the request within **2 business days**. We verify:

- The competitor is on the approved list
- The product is identical
- The price is live and qualifies

### Step 4: Refund
If approved, the difference is refunded to your **original payment 
method** within **5–7 business days**. You'll receive a confirmation 
email with the amount.

## Price Match Limits

- **One price match per order** — subsequent lower prices don't trigger 
  additional refunds
- **One price match per product, per customer, per 30 days** — prevents 
  abuse
- **Maximum refund**: $50 per item
- **Minimum difference**: $2.00 — differences under $2 are not processed

## Refunds vs. Store Credit

Price match refunds are issued to your **original payment method**, 
just like a return refund. We do not offer store credit for price matches.

For payment-method-specific refund timelines, see the 
[Return & Refund Policy](./return_refund_policy.md).

## Price Drops on voltnest.com

If the price of an item you bought **drops on voltnest.com** within 14 
days of delivery, we'll automatically refund the difference — no request 
needed.

You'll receive an email within 24 hours of the price drop with the 
refund confirmation. This is our "self price match" and applies 
automatically to all customers.

**Does not apply to**:

- Promotional prices (flash sales, seasonal)
- Coupon-applied prices
- Bundled pricing
- Prices after the 14-day window

## Common Questions

**Can I price match if the competitor is out of stock?**  
No. The competitor must have the item in stock at the price you're 
claiming. We only match live, available prices.

**What if the competitor raises the price after I submit?**  
We verify at the moment of review. If the price has changed, the request 
is denied. Submit a fresh request if the price drops again within the 
window.

**Can I price match a product I bought on Amazon from VoltNest?**  
No. Price matching only applies to purchases made directly on 
voltnest.com.

**Do I have to return the item first?**  
No. You keep the item and receive the difference as a refund.

**What if the competitor is running a "10% off everything" sale?**  
Promotional prices don't qualify. Only standard retail prices are matched.

**Can I combine price match with a coupon or promo code?**  
Yes, but the price match compares the **competitor's price** to your 
**pre-coupon price**. Coupons don't affect price match eligibility.

**Does price match apply to shipping costs?**  
No. We match the product price only, not shipping.

## Abuse Prevention

We reserve the right to deny price match requests that appear to be:

- Repeated or excessive (more than 3 in 90 days)
- Based on fraudulent screenshots
- From resellers or bulk purchasers
- Aimed at gaming the system (e.g., fabricated competitor pages)

Customers found abusing the policy may have price match privileges 
suspended.
