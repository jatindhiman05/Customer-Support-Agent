# Shipping Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

This policy covers all aspects of shipping for orders placed on 
voltnest.com — rates, delivery windows, order processing, tracking, 
lost packages, and international shipping. It applies to all customers 
worldwide.

## Order Processing Time

Orders are processed and packed at our Memphis, Tennessee distribution 
center. Processing time depends on when the order is placed:

| Order Placed | Ships |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday before 2:00 PM EST | Same business day |
| Monday–Friday after 2:00 PM EST | Next business day |
| Saturday or Sunday | Next business day (Monday) |
| US federal holidays | Next business day after the holiday |

**Processing time is separate from shipping time.** A 2-day shipping 
method means 2 days in transit *after* the order ships.

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

We offer three shipping speeds within the continental United States:

| Method | Delivery Time | Cost | Free Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $4.99 | Free on orders over $35 |
| Express | 2–3 business days | $12.99 | — |
| Overnight | Next business day | $24.99 | — |

### Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories

Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the US Virgin Islands 
takes **7–10 business days** via Standard. Express and Overnight are 
**not available** for these destinations.

## International Shipping

VoltNest ships to 12 international markets. Delivery times and rates:

| Region | Countries | Delivery Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canada | Canada | 7–10 business days | $14.99 |
| UK & EU | United Kingdom, all EU member states | 10–14 business days | $19.99 |
| Asia-Pacific | Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore | 12–18 business days | $24.99 |
| South Asia | India | 12–18 business days | $24.99 |

**Free shipping does not apply to international orders.**

### Customs, Duties, and Taxes

For international orders:

- Import duties, customs fees, and local taxes are the **customer's responsibility**
- These are collected by the destination country's customs agency, not VoltNest
- VoltNest does not estimate or prepay these fees
- If a package is refused due to unpaid duties, the customer is responsible 
  for return shipping

### International Shipping Restrictions

Due to carrier regulations, we **cannot ship** the following 
internationally:

- Lithium batteries (VoltBank 20K power bank)
- Orders over $500 USD to any country
- Orders to PO boxes outside the United States

## Tracking Your Order

Every order receives a tracking number by email within **24 hours** of 
shipping. Tracking is available through:

1. The confirmation email (direct carrier link)
2. Your account at voltnest.com → Orders
3. The carrier's website directly

**If tracking hasn't updated in 7 days**, contact support — we'll open 
an investigation with the carrier within 48 hours.

## Lost, Delayed, or Damaged Packages

### Lost Packages

A package is considered **lost** if:

- Tracking shows no update for **10 business days** (domestic)
- Tracking shows no update for **21 business days** (international)

For lost packages, we will:

1. Open a formal investigation with the carrier
2. Offer a full refund **or** free replacement (your choice)
3. Complete the process within **10 business days**

### Delayed Packages

Delays caused by weather, carrier issues, or customs are outside our 
control. We do not issue refunds for delays, but we will:

- Provide updated ETAs when available
- Offer a **10% discount** on your next order if the delay exceeds 
  5 business days beyond the estimate

### Damaged Packages

If your package arrives visibly damaged:

1. **Photograph the package and contents before opening fully**
2. Report within **48 hours of delivery** to support@voltnest.com
3. We ship a replacement immediately (before receiving the damaged item)
4. No return shipping cost to you

## Shipping Restrictions

We cannot ship to:

- PO boxes over $200 USD (fraud prevention)
- Freight forwarders
- Military installations other than APO/FPO addresses
- Countries not listed in our international shipping table

## Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping addresses. 
If an order ships to an incorrect address:

- **Customer error**: We can attempt a carrier intercept for **$15**, 
  if the package hasn't been delivered. Success is not guaranteed.
- **VoltNest error**: We reship at no cost, on priority shipping.

Address changes **after** an order ships are not possible through the 
website. Contact support immediately if you need to update.
