# Return & Refund Policy

> **Category**: policies  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

This policy covers how customers can return items, receive refunds, and 
handle exchanges for orders placed on voltnest.com. It applies to all 
products and all customers worldwide. For damaged or defective items, 
see the [Warranty Policy](./warranty_policy.md) instead.

## Return Window

Customers may return most items within **30 calendar days** of the 
delivery date shown on the carrier's tracking page.

**Important**: The window starts on the day the package is marked 
**"Delivered"** by the carrier — not the order date, not the ship date.

Returns received after 30 days are not eligible for a refund. Contact 
support if you have an exceptional circumstance (hospitalization, 
travel, natural disaster) — we'll evaluate on a case-by-case basis.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a full refund, items must be:

- Unused and in original condition
- In original packaging with all inserts, manuals, and accessories
- Free of cosmetic damage, scratches, or wear
- Complete — electronics must include all cables and accessories

**Items that fail any of these conditions** may receive a **partial 
refund of 50–80%** at VoltNest's discretion, or be returned to the 
customer at their cost.

## Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect the returned item:

| Step | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Carrier transit to our facility | 3–7 business days |
| Inspection | 1–2 business days |
| Refund approval | 1 business day |
| Refund processing to payment method | 5–7 business days |

**Total from ship-back to refund in your account: 10–17 business days.**

Refunds are issued to the **original payment method only**. We cannot 
issue refunds to a different card, bank account, or as cash.

## Return Shipping Costs

Who pays return shipping depends on the reason for the return:

| Reason | Who Pays | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Changed mind / no longer needed | Customer | $6.99 (US), actual cost (intl) |
| Wrong item shipped | VoltNest | $0 |
| Damaged in transit | VoltNest | $0 |
| Defective on arrival | VoltNest | $0 |
| Wrong size / didn't fit | Customer | $6.99 (US) |
| Item no longer wanted after 14 days | Customer | $6.99 (US) |

For customer-paid returns, we deduct the return shipping fee from the 
refund amount. No separate payment is required.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following **cannot be returned** for hygiene, legal, or fraud 
reasons:

- Gift cards
- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Personalized or custom-engraved items
- Items marked **"Final Sale"** at checkout
- Ear tips and hygiene products if the packaging has been opened
- Products returned without an RMA number

## How to Start a Return

1. Log in to your VoltNest account
2. Go to **Orders → Select order → "Return or Exchange"**
3. Choose a reason from the dropdown
4. Print the prepaid label (if provided) or the return address label
5. Pack the item securely with all accessories and inserts
6. Drop off at any carrier location

You'll receive two emails:

- **Confirmation** when we receive the package
- **Refund issued** when the refund has been processed

**Do not ship returns without an RMA number.** Unmarked returns may be 
refused or lost, and VoltNest is not responsible for them.

## RMA Numbers

Every return requires a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)** 
number, generated automatically when you initiate the return online. 
The RMA number:

- Is valid for **14 days** from issuance
- Must be written on the outside of the return package
- Is tied to a specific order and item

Expired RMAs require a new return request.

## Exchanges

We do **not** offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item:

1. Return the original item for a refund
2. Place a new order for the item you want

This ensures the fastest possible delivery of your replacement. During 
peak seasons, exchanges may take longer than a direct reorder.

## Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or wrong item:

1. **Report within 48 hours of delivery**
2. Include photos of the item and packaging
3. Contact support@voltnest.com with your order ID

We will:

- Ship a replacement immediately (before receiving the return)
- Provide a prepaid return label
- Cover all shipping costs

**Do not initiate a return through the online portal** for damaged or 
wrong items — contact support first so we can expedite the replacement.

## International Returns

International customers follow the same 30-day return window. However:

- Customer-paid return shipping is the **actual carrier cost** (typically 
  $25–$60 depending on country)
- Customs duties and import taxes are **not refundable** by VoltNest
- International returns must be shipped within **14 days** of RMA issuance
- Refunds may take **3–5 additional business days** for international 
  banking

For damaged or wrong items shipped internationally, VoltNest provides 
a prepaid label or reimburses reasonable return shipping.

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

| Payment Method | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Credit/Debit card | 5–7 business days after approval |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days after approval |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | 5–7 business days after approval |
| Klarna / Afterpay | Adjusted against remaining installments |
| Gift card | Immediate (new gift card issued) |

**Bank processing times vary.** VoltNest issues refunds immediately upon 
approval; the remaining time depends on your bank or payment provider.

## Partial Refunds

A partial refund may apply when:

- Item is returned used, damaged, or incomplete
- Original packaging is missing or damaged
- Accessories are missing
- Item shows signs of wear beyond inspection

Partial refunds range from **50–80%** of the original purchase price, 
determined at VoltNest's discretion after inspection.

## Denied Returns

A return may be denied if:

- It falls outside the 30-day window
- The item is on the non-returnable list
- The RMA number is missing or expired
- The item is materially different from what was shipped

Denied returns are shipped back to the customer at the customer's cost. 
If the customer declines the return shipment, the item is disposed of 
after 30 days.
