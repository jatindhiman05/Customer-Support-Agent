# Shipping Terms Glossary

> **Category**: glossary  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Definitions of shipping and delivery terms used across VoltNest's 
knowledge base. Terms are listed alphabetically. Each definition is 
1–3 sentences and links to the source document where the term is used.

## A

### AWB (Air Waybill)

A document issued by a carrier for international air freight. Contains 
the shipment details and tracking number. VoltNest provides AWB 
tracking for all international orders via DHL and international 
postal partners.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### Address Verification

The process where a carrier confirms the recipient's address is valid 
and deliverable before shipping. Prevents failed deliveries. If 
verification fails, the order may be held or cancelled.

*Used in*: [Modify Order Guide](../order_management/modify_order_guide.md)

## B

### Bill of Lading

A legal document between the shipper and carrier that details the 
type, quantity, and destination of goods. Primarily for freight. Not 
typically issued to individual VoltNest customers, but referenced in 
carrier investigations.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Bulk Shipment

Multiple orders shipped together to a single address or region. Not 
used for individual VoltNest customer orders — each order ships 
separately.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## C

### Carrier

The logistics company that physically transports your package. 
VoltNest uses UPS, USPS, FedEx, and DHL depending on the destination 
and shipping method. See [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Carrier Intercept

A request made to the carrier to stop or redirect a package in 
transit. VoltNest can request an intercept for address changes on 
shipped orders for a **$15 fee**. Success is not guaranteed (~70% 
success rate if requested early).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[Modify Order Guide](../order_management/modify_order_guide.md), 
[Cancel Order Guide](../order_management/cancel_order_guide.md)

### Commercial Invoice

A customs document that lists the contents, value, and origin of 
goods in an international shipment. Required for most international 
orders. VoltNest generates this automatically.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Confirmed Delivery

A delivery where the carrier has obtained a signature or photo as 
proof of delivery. Common for high-value orders. If you didn't sign 
but tracking shows "Delivered," see 
[Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md).

*Used in*: [Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md)

### Customs

The government agency in each country that inspects imports and 
collects duties and taxes. Customs can delay, hold, or refuse a 
package. See [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### Customs Clearance

The process where a package is reviewed by customs and released for 
delivery. Typically 1–10 business days depending on country. Long 
delays may indicate additional documents are needed.

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### Customs Duty

A tax imposed by a country on imported goods. Paid by the customer 
upon delivery in most countries. VoltNest does not prepay or reimburse 
customs duties. See [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

## D

### Delivery Attempt

A carrier's effort to deliver a package to the recipient's address. 
If no one is available (or a signature is required), the attempt 
fails and the carrier may try again or return the package.

*Used in*: [Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md), 
[Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Delivery Confirmation

Proof that a package was delivered — usually a signature, photo, or 
scan at the destination address. Provided by the carrier.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Delivery Exception

A carrier status indicating an unexpected issue — weather delay, 
mechanical failure, inaccessible address, or customs hold. Most 
exceptions resolve within 5 business days.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md), 
[Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md)

### DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

An international shipping term where the shipper pays all duties and 
taxes upfront. **VoltNest does not offer DDP.** Our international 
orders are DDU (see below).

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### DDU / DAP (Delivered Duty Unpaid / Delivered At Place)

An international shipping term where the recipient pays duties and 
taxes upon delivery. This is how all VoltNest international orders 
ship.

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md), 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Distribution Center (DC)

A warehouse where orders are packed and shipped. VoltNest's 
distribution center is in **Memphis, Tennessee**. See 
[About VoltNest](../company/about.md).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[About VoltNest](../company/about.md)

### Domestic Shipping

Shipping within the same country (for VoltNest, within the United 
States). Domestic orders have faster delivery and lower rates than 
international orders.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## E

### ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival)

The carrier's estimate of when your package will be delivered. ETAs 
are estimates, not guarantees. They can change due to weather, 
customs, or carrier delays.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Export Declaration

A document required by US Customs for international shipments over 
a certain value. Includes a description of goods, value, and 
destination. VoltNest files this automatically.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Express Shipping

A faster, more expensive shipping option. VoltNest Express delivers 
in **2–3 business days** within the US for **$12.99**. See 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md).

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## F

### Freight Forwarder

A company that consolidates shipments and arranges international 
transport on behalf of the shipper. **VoltNest does not ship to 
freight forwarders** — orders to them may be cancelled.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

## G

### GST (Goods and Services Tax)

A value-added tax used in Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, 
and India. International customers may pay GST upon delivery. See 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md).

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

## H

### HTS Code (Harmonized Tariff Schedule)

A numerical code that classifies goods for customs purposes. 
Determines the duty rate applied to a product. VoltNest assigns 
correct HTS codes on all international shipments.

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### Handling Time

The time between order confirmation and carrier pickup. VoltNest's 
handling time is **1–2 business days** for domestic orders. Separate 
from shipping (transit) time.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## I

### In Transit

A carrier status indicating a package is moving through the carrier's 
network. Multiple "In Transit" updates appear as the package passes 
through sorting hubs.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### International Shipping

Shipping from one country to another. VoltNest ships internationally 
to **12 markets** — see [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md). 
VoltBank 20K cannot ship internationally due to lithium battery 
regulations.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

## L

### Label Created

A carrier status meaning the shipping label was printed but the 
package hasn't been picked up yet. Usually updates to "In Transit" 
within 1–2 business days.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Lithium Battery Restriction

A carrier and regulatory rule that restricts shipping lithium-ion 
batteries internationally. Affects **VoltBank 20K** — it cannot ship 
outside the US.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md), 
[International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

### Lost Package

A package with no tracking update for **7+ business days (domestic)** 
or **14+ business days (international)**. VoltNest opens a carrier 
investigation and issues a refund or replacement if confirmed lost.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md), 
[Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md)

## M

### Manifest

A document listing all packages on a carrier's vehicle or in a 
shipment batch. Used by carriers for reconciliation.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## O

### Order Processing Time

See **Handling Time** above.

### Origin Scan

A carrier status indicating the first scan of the package at the 
carrier's facility. Confirms the carrier has physically received the 
package.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### Overnight Shipping

A shipping option delivering the next business day within the US. 
VoltNest Overnight is **$24.99** and requires ordering before 
**2:00 PM EST**.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## P

### Package Intercept

See **Carrier Intercept** above.

### Packing Slip

A document included in the package that lists items shipped. Does not 
include pricing when the order is marked as a gift.

*Used in*: [Modify Order Guide](../order_management/modify_order_guide.md)

### Pickup

The carrier's collection of a package from VoltNest's distribution 
center. Once picked up, the package status changes to "Shipped."

*Used in*: [Order Status Guide](../order_management/order_status_guide.md)

### Proof of Delivery (POD)

Carrier documentation confirming delivery — signature, photo, or scan. 
Used for delivery disputes.

*Used in*: [Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md), 
[Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

## R

### Refused Delivery

When the recipient declines a package at the door. The package 
returns to VoltNest. Shipping fees are not refunded, and a 15% 
restocking fee may apply for international refusals.

*Used in*: [Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md), 
[Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md)

### Restocking Fee

A fee charged when a returned item requires repackaging or 
restocking. **VoltNest does not charge restocking fees** except for 
refused international deliveries (15%).

*Used in*: [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md), 
[Cancellation Policy](../policies/cancellation_policy.md)

### Return to Sender (RTS)

A carrier status meaning the package is being returned to VoltNest. 
Common causes: refused delivery, undeliverable address, multiple 
failed attempts.

*Used in*: [Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md), 
[Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md)

### RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization)

A number issued when a return is initiated. Required for all returns. 
Valid for **14 days**. Must be written on the outside of the return 
package.

*Used in*: [Return & Refund Policy](../policies/return_refund_policy.md)

## S

### Shipping Label

A printed document with the recipient address, barcode, and tracking 
number. Attached to the package before pickup.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Signature Required

A delivery requirement where the recipient (or someone at the address) 
must sign for the package. Common for high-value orders.

*Used in*: [Delivery Issues](../order_management/delivery_issues.md)

### Standard Shipping

VoltNest's default shipping method. **5–7 business days** within the 
US, **$4.99**, free on orders over **$35**.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## T

### Tracking Number

A unique alphanumeric identifier assigned by the carrier to a package. 
VoltNest sends tracking numbers by email within 24 hours of shipping.

*Used in*: [Track Shipment Guide](../order_management/track_shipment_guide.md), 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Transit Time

The time a package spends in the carrier's network — from pickup to 
delivery. Separate from handling time.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## V

### VAT (Value Added Tax)

A consumption tax used in the UK, EU, and many other countries. 
International customers may pay VAT upon delivery. Rates range from 
**17% to 27%** depending on the country.

*Used in*: [International FAQ](../faqs/international_faq.md)

## W

### Waybill

Another name for the shipping document that accompanies a package. 
Contains sender, recipient, contents, and tracking information.

*Used in*: [Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Wholesale Shipping

Bulk shipping for business orders. Handled through 
partners@voltnest.com, not standard checkout.

*Used in*: [General FAQ](../faqs/general_faq.md)
