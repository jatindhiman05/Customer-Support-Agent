# Product Terms Glossary

> **Category**: glossary  
> **Audience**: customer, agent  
> **Last updated**: 2026-01-15

Definitions of audio, power, and electronics terms used across 
VoltNest's knowledge base. Terms are listed alphabetically. Each 
definition is 1–3 sentences and links to the source document where 
the term is used.

## A

### AAC (Advanced Audio Codec)

A Bluetooth audio codec developed by Apple and used across iOS devices. 
Provides good quality at lower bitrates than SBC. VoltBuds Pro and 
VoltBoom support AAC automatically with iPhones, iPads, and Macs.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### ANC (Active Noise Cancellation)

A technology that uses microphones and signal processing to actively 
cancel ambient noise. **VoltBuds Pro does NOT have ANC** — it has 
passive noise isolation only (through ear tip seal). ANC models are 
planned for a future product line.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### aptX

A Bluetooth audio codec developed by Qualcomm. Provides higher 
quality than SBC on supported Android devices. VoltBuds Pro supports 
aptX for compatible Android phones.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### A2DP (Advanced Audio Distribution Profile)

The Bluetooth profile used for streaming high-quality stereo audio 
from one device to another. Used for music playback. Different from 
HFP, which is used for calls.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

## B

### Battery Cycle

One complete charge-discharge cycle of a battery. A cycle is counted 
when the battery goes from 100% to 0% and back to 100% — partial 
charges count proportionally. VoltBank 20K is rated for **500+ cycles**.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

### Battery Swelling

Physical expansion of a lithium-ion battery, usually from damage, 
overheating, or age. **Safety-critical.** If any VoltNest product's 
battery swells, stop using it immediately and contact support.

*Used in*: [Device Not Charging](../troubleshooting/device_not_charging.md), 
[Sensitive Topics](../escalation/sensitive_topics.md)

### Bluetooth 5.3

The Bluetooth specification version used by VoltBuds Pro and 
VoltBoom. Offers improved range, speed, and power efficiency over 
Bluetooth 5.0. Backward-compatible with all older Bluetooth versions.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

### Bluetooth Range

The maximum distance between a Bluetooth device and its paired 
source. VoltBuds Pro: **10 meters (33 feet)**. VoltBoom: **30 meters 
(98 feet)**. Walls and obstacles reduce range.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md)

## C

### CE Mark

A certification mark indicating a product meets European Union 
safety, health, and environmental standards. Required for electronics 
sold in the EU. All VoltNest electronics carry CE certification.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

### Codec

Short for "coder-decoder." A method of encoding and decoding audio 
data for transmission over Bluetooth. Common codecs: **SBC** 
(universal), **AAC** (Apple), **aptX** (Qualcomm). Higher-quality 
codecs use more bandwidth.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Cycling (Battery)

See **Battery Cycle** above.

## D

### DAC (Digital-to-Analog Converter)

A chip that converts digital audio into analog signals for speakers. 
Every Bluetooth speaker and pair of earbuds includes a DAC. Higher-
quality DACs produce cleaner sound.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)

### Driver (Speaker Driver)

The component that produces sound — essentially a small speaker 
inside earbuds or headphones. Measured in millimeters. VoltBuds Pro 
uses **11 mm dynamic drivers**.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)

## E

### ENC (Environmental Noise Cancellation)

A microphone technology that filters background noise during calls. 
Unlike ANC, ENC affects only the microphone input (how you sound to 
the other person), not what you hear. **VoltBuds Pro has dual-mic 
ENC** for clear calls.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)

## F

### FCC (Federal Communications Commission)

The US government agency that regulates wireless devices. **FCC 
certification** is required for any wireless device sold in the US. 
All VoltNest Bluetooth products carry FCC certification.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

### Firmware

Software built into a hardware device that controls its behavior. 
VoltNest products receive firmware updates through the VoltNest app. 
Keeping firmware current fixes bugs and improves performance.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Frequency Response

The range of audio frequencies a speaker or driver can reproduce, 
measured in Hertz (Hz). VoltBoom: **60 Hz – 20 kHz**. Human hearing 
ranges from about 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

## G

### GaN (Gallium Nitride)

A semiconductor material used in modern compact chargers. More 
energy-efficient than traditional silicon, allowing chargers to be 
**smaller, cooler, and more efficient** at the same power output. 
VoltCharge 65W uses GaN technology.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

### GaN Charger

A wall charger built with Gallium Nitride semiconductors. Typically 
40–50% smaller than equivalent silicon chargers.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

## H

### HFP (Hands-Free Profile)

The Bluetooth profile used for phone calls. Lower bandwidth than 
A2DP (which handles music), so call audio quality sounds different 
from music. Normal and expected — not a defect.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Hz (Hertz)

A unit of frequency — one cycle per second. Used to measure audio 
frequencies (e.g., 60 Hz bass, 20 kHz treble) and refresh rates.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

## I

### IP Rating (Ingress Protection)

A standard (IEC 60529) that rates a device's resistance to dust and 
water. Format: **IPXY**, where X = dust protection (0–6), Y = water 
protection (0–8). Example: IP67 = dust-tight + water-resistant to 
1m for 30 minutes.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)

### IP6X

The highest dust-resistance rating — completely dust-tight. VoltBoom 
has an IP6X dust rating.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

### IP67

A combined rating: dust-tight (6) + water-resistant to **1 meter 
depth for 30 minutes** (7). VoltBoom has an IP67 rating. Suitable 
for pools, rain, and accidental drops in water.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### IPX5

A water-resistance rating where the X means "not rated for dust" and 
the 5 means "resistant to water jets" (e.g., sweat, light rain). 
**Not submersible.** VoltBuds Pro has IPX5 — safe for workouts but 
not swimming.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

## L

### Latency

The delay between an audio source and when you hear it. Bluetooth 
latency is typically **100–300 milliseconds** — inherent to the 
technology, not a defect. Wired connections have near-zero latency.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Lithium-Ion (Li-ion)

The rechargeable battery chemistry used in all VoltNest battery 
products (VoltBuds Pro case, VoltBoom, VoltBank 20K). High energy 
density, lightweight, and safe when used correctly. Regulated for 
air transport.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md), 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

## M

### mAh (Milliamp-Hour)

A unit of battery capacity — how much energy a battery can store. 
VoltBank 20K has **20,000 mAh** capacity. Higher mAh = more charges 
before recharging.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

### MagSafe

Apple's magnetic attachment system for iPhones and accessories. 
VoltShield cases are MagSafe-compatible with a built-in magnet array. 
Magnet strength is measured in Newtons (N) — VoltShield is rated at 
**12N**, matching Apple's official cases.

*Used in*: [Phone Case Product Page](../products/phone_case.md)

### Multi-Point (Multipoint Pairing)

A Bluetooth feature that allows a device to be connected to **two 
sources at once** (e.g., phone + laptop). VoltBuds Pro supports 
multipoint with up to 2 devices. VoltBoom does not support multipoint.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md)

### Multi-Speaker Pairing

A VoltBoom feature that links multiple speakers for synchronized 
playback. Two modes: **Stereo** (2 speakers, left/right channels) 
and **Party** (up to 100 speakers, mono). Different from multipoint.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md)

## N

### Noise Isolation (Passive)

Blocking external sound physically, without electronics — through 
ear tip seal or over-ear padding. VoltBuds Pro uses passive noise 
isolation. Different from ANC (active).

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

## P

### Passive Radiator

A speaker component that enhances bass without using power. Works 
by vibrating in response to pressure changes inside the speaker 
enclosure. VoltBoom has dual passive radiators for deep bass.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Pass-Through Charging

Charging a power bank while it simultaneously charges another device. 
VoltBank 20K supports pass-through charging, though it slows the 
bank's own recharge.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

### PPS (Programmable Power Supply)

A USB-PD extension that allows fine-grained voltage and current 
adjustment for optimal charging. Required for Samsung Super Fast 
Charging and some other fast-charge standards. VoltCharge 65W 
supports PPS.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

## Q

### QC 4.0+ (Quick Charge)

Qualcomm's fast-charging protocol. Rapidly charges compatible Android 
devices. VoltCharge 65W supports QC 4.0+ and earlier Quick Charge 
versions.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

### Qi (Wireless Charging)

A universal wireless charging standard pronounced "chee." Any Qi-
certified charger can charge any Qi-compatible device. VoltBuds Pro 
charging case supports Qi wireless charging.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)

## R

### RMS (Root Mean Square)

A measure of continuous power output, in watts. More meaningful than 
"peak" or "max" wattage because it reflects sustained output. 
VoltBoom outputs **20W RMS**.

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

### RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances)

An EU directive restricting the use of certain hazardous materials 
(lead, mercury, cadmium, etc.) in electronics. All VoltNest 
electronics are RoHS-compliant.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

## S

### SBC (Subband Codec)

The default Bluetooth audio codec. Universally supported but 
lower-quality than AAC or aptX. Used as a fallback when both devices 
don't share a higher-quality codec.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md)

### Stereo Mode (VoltBoom)

A multi-speaker mode using **2 VoltBoom speakers** for true left/
right channel stereo. Different from Party mode (which links up to 
100 speakers in mono).

*Used in*: [Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md), 
[Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md)

### SW (Software Version)

See **Firmware** above.

## T

### TOTP (Time-Based One-Time Password)

A 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app that refreshes every 
30 seconds. Used for 2FA. Codes fail if your phone's clock is off by 
more than 30 seconds.

*Used in*: [Enable 2FA](../account/enable_2fa.md), 
[Login Issues](../troubleshooting/login_issues.md)

## U

### UL (Underwriters Laboratories)

A US-based safety certification organization. **UL certification** 
means a product has been tested for electrical safety. All VoltNest 
charging products are UL-certified.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md), 
[Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

### UN38.3

An international standard for the safe transport of lithium 
batteries. All VoltNest lithium battery products are UN38.3 
certified. Required for air transport and shipping compliance.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md), 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### USB-A

The classic rectangular USB port. Slower than USB-C and cannot 
deliver high wattages. VoltCharge 65W has 1 USB-A port (max 18W) for 
legacy devices.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md)

### USB-C

A modern USB connector that's reversible, compact, and capable of 
high power (up to 240W) and data (up to 40 Gbps). Used by most 
VoltNest products for charging and data.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md), 
[Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

### USB-PD (USB Power Delivery)

A fast-charging standard that allows up to **240W** over USB-C. 
Requires both a PD-capable charger and a PD-capable device. 
VoltCharge 65W supports USB-PD 3.0.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md), 
[Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md)

## W

### Watt (W)

A unit of power — the rate at which energy is used or produced. 
Chargers are rated in watts (e.g., 65W), speakers in watts (e.g., 
20W RMS). Higher watts = more power, but not always better.

*Used in*: [USB-C Charger Product Page](../products/usb_c_charger.md), 
[Bluetooth Speaker Product Page](../products/bluetooth_speaker.md)

### Wh (Watt-Hour)

A unit of energy — the total energy a battery can store. VoltBank 
20K is rated at **74 Wh**. Used by airlines and regulators: batteries 
under **100 Wh** are typically allowed in carry-on luggage.

*Used in*: [Power Bank Product Page](../products/power_bank.md), 
[Shipping Policy](../policies/shipping_policy.md)

### Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz vs. 5 GHz)

Two frequency bands used by Wi-Fi networks. **2.4 GHz** has longer 
range but slower speeds and interferes with Bluetooth. **5 GHz** is 
faster but shorter range. Switching to 5 GHz can reduce Bluetooth 
audio dropouts.

*Used in*: [Audio Quality Issues](../troubleshooting/audio_quality_issues.md), 
[Bluetooth Pairing](../troubleshooting/bluetooth_pairing.md)

### Wireless Charging

Charging without cables, using electromagnetic induction. See **Qi** 
above.

*Used in*: [Wireless Earbuds Product Page](../products/wireless_earbuds.md)
